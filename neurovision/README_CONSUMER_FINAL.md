## 🔬 Clinical Foundation: Concussion Clinical Mastery (CCM) Integration

**Based on:** *Concussion Clinical Mastery* (Lewis, 2026) — Complete reference framework for phenotype-based rehabilitation

### CCM Principles Integrated:

1. **VOMS-Inspired Symptom Provocation**
   - Before/after symptom scales (0-10 rating)
   - 6 symptoms: headache, dizziness, nausea, fogginess, eye strain, cognitive fog
   - Delta calculation: >2 = mild, >5 = moderate, >10 = severe (clinical action thresholds)

2. **Phenotype-Based Rehab Mapping (CCM Module 7)**
   - **Vestibular/Oculomotor phenotype** → VOR exercises, gaze stabilization
   - **Cortical/Attentional phenotype** → Cognitive rehab, attention training
   - **Autonomic phenotype** → Breathwork, ANS balance exercises
   - **Dyslexic oculomotor phenotype** → Saccade/fixation training BEFORE phonics

3. **Multi-Modal Assessment**
   - Gaze (holding vs. shifting)
   - Blink (rate, duration, frequency)
   - Pupil (flash reflex, ANS integrity)
   - Head pose (VOR proxy, nystagmus detection)
   - Balance (proxy via head stability)
   - Symptoms (provocation deltas)

4. **Compliance & Professional Standards**
   - **NOT diagnostic** — Wellness exploration only
   - **Strong referrals** — Severe symptoms → Concussion Australia, neuro-optometry, neuropsych
   - **Evidence-based** — Cite 2023-2025 research only (Ma et al., Snegireva et al., AAN 2023, dyslexia sensorimotor hypothesis)
   - **Medicolegal safeguards** — Disclaimers, no efficacy claims, emergency resources

5. **Gamified Provocation Loop™**
   - Engaging challenges (driving simulation, reading task, pupil flash)
   - Real-time feedback (quadrant lag visualization, blink counter)
   - Immediate insights ("Your eyes lag in this area")
   - Positive reinforcement (celebrate improvements, suggest next steps)

### CCM Objectives Met:

✅ **Identify phenotypes** (vestibular/oculomotor vs. cortical/attentional vs. autonomic)
✅ **Map to interventions** (VOR exercises, cognitive rehab, breathwork, neuro-optometry)
✅ **Track longitudinally** (export data, observe trends over weeks)
✅ **Refer appropriately** (severe symptoms → professional care)
✅ **Empower users** (self-knowledge → action)

## 🏆 Proprietary IP & Algorithms (Patent-Pending)

### 6 World-First Algorithms:

#### 1️⃣ **Gaze Holding Score™** (Vestibular/Oculomotor Assessment)
**What It Measures:**
- Smooth pursuit gain (track moving targets)
- Fixation drift (hold steady gaze)
- VOR gain (track during head movement)
- Catch-up saccades (compensatory jerks)
- Nystagmus detection (jerky eye movements)

**Consumer Insight:**
> "Your eyes struggle to hold steady on moving targets (score: 62/100). This may contribute to car sickness, sports issues, or screen headaches. Try VOR exercises or consult a vestibular therapist."

**Wearable Application:**

- Sports performance: Track gaze holding during rapid head turns
- Concussion baseline: Pre-season assessment for athletes

#### 2️⃣ **Gaze Shifting Score™** (Cortical/Attentional Assessment)
**What It Measures:**
- Saccade latency (time to shift focus)
- Saccade accuracy (land on target correctly)
- Antisaccade errors (inhibitory control)
- Rapid scanning speed (visual search efficiency)

**Consumer Insight:**
> "Your eyes are slow to shift between targets (score: 58/100). This may contribute to ADHD-like attention issues, reading fatigue, or task-switching difficulties. Consider cognitive exercises or neuropsych eval."

**Wearable Application:**
- ADHD detection: Flag inhibitory control deficits
- Reading in VR: Optimize text rendering for slow saccades
- Gaming performance: Detect attentional lapses
