## 🔬 Scientific Foundation

### VOMS Protocol (Mucha et al. 2014)
- Vestibular/Ocular Motor Screening with 89% sensitivity for concussion
- Symptom provocation methodology (before/after scales)
- Used in 80% of US sports medicine clinics

### King-Devick Test (Galetta et al. 2011)
- Rapid number reading test with 100% sensitivity (when baseline available)
- Inspiration for reading-based assessment (fully differentiated design)

### Dyslexia Sensorimotor Research (2024-2025)
- Emerging evidence for primary oculomotor deficits in some dyslexic readers
- Fixation instability correlates with reading fluency (r=0.68)
- Regression count predictive of intervention response

### Multi-Modal Assessment (CCM Module 7)
- Pupillometry as cognitive load proxy
- Head pose tracking as VOR substitute (MediaPipe)
- Balance self-report for vestibular integration

### Smartphone Pupillometry Research (2024-2025)
- **Master et al. (2024):** Smartphone pupillometry shows 82% sensitivity for concussion detection
- **Truong et al. (2023):** Pupillary light reflex abnormalities in mTBI persist 3+ months
- **Ellis et al. (2025):** ANS dysfunction biomarkers via pupil flash metrics
- **Normal ranges:** Latency 200-300ms, Amplitude 20-40%, Recovery 3-5s
- **PCS markers:** Reduced amplitude (<20%), delayed latency (>350ms), prolonged recovery (>7s)

## 🏠 Home Detection Hurdles & Mitigation Strategies

### Challenge 1: Accuracy Limitations
**Issue:** Webcam-based tracking has 1-3° noise (vs 0.1° in research systems)

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on **relative metrics** (deltas, trends) not absolute precision
- Multi-modal fusion (pupil + head + symptoms) improves signal
- Gamification increases engagement → more reliable data

### Challenge 2: Provocation Risks
**Issue:** Symptom aggravation during assessment could worsen concussion symptoms

**Mitigation:**
- **Pre-assessment screening:** Consent checkbox excludes severe symptoms (>6/10)
- **Real-time monitoring:** Auto-stop if delta >10 or any symptom ≥8
- **Tiered alerts:** Yellow (moderate) and red (severe) with clinical action steps
- **Direct referral pathway:** Link to Concussion Australia provider finder

### Challenge 3: Regulatory Considerations
**Issue:** Cannot make diagnostic claims without FDA/TGA clearance

**Mitigation:**
- **Wellness framing:** "Assessment tool" not "diagnostic device"
- **Strong disclaimers:** Every page states "NOT DIAGNOSTIC"
- **Clinical pathway:** Funnel high-risk users to professionals
- **IRB-ready data:** Supports research use without diagnostic claims

### Challenge 4: Adherence & Engagement
**Issue:** Home users may skip steps or provide inaccurate self-report

**Mitigation:**
- **Gamification:** Levels, badges, scores increase motivation
- **Social sharing:** X/Twitter posts create accountability
- **Empathetic UX:** "Many people with headaches notice..." reduces stigma
- **Session logging:** Detects incomplete assessments

### Challenge 5: Privacy & Data Security
**Issue:** Sensitive health data must be protected

**Mitigation:**
- **Local-first:** No server required, runs entirely in browser
- **Anonymized sessions:** Session IDs are hashed, no PII required
- **User-controlled export:** Data never leaves device unless explicitly downloaded
- **GDPR/HIPAA-ready:** Architecture supports compliance (anonymization, consent, export)

### Access Expansion
- **Rural/Remote Areas:** No specialists within 50+ miles

- **Frequent Monitoring:** Concussion recovery needs weekly tracking (impossible at $300/visit)
