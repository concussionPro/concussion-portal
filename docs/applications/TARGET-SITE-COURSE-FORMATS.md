# Target-Site CPD/Short-Course Formats — Verified Research

**Purpose:** Calibrate the length, format, and structure of a free concussion lead-course to what the professional bodies CEA targets actually expect and endorse.

**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Method:** WebFetch + curl on specific URLs only. WebSearch/browser unavailable. Every factual claim below carries the URL actually fetched and a quote. Anything not fetched successfully is marked **UNVERIFIED** — do not treat it as fact.

---

## HEADLINE FINDING (read this first)

**The dominant, expected shape of an endorsable CPD short course on these sites is ~1 hour of content with a quiz/assessment and a downloadable certificate — NOT a multi-hour course.** The clearest live precedent (OT Potential, which already sells to NZ OTs) runs "1–1.5 hours" per unit, awards "1 hr" of CEU, and uses a **short quiz + instant PDF certificate** on every course. The NZ endorsement maths (REPs) is built around this: **~1 hour of learning ≈ 0.5–0.6 CPD points**, and **whether there is an assessment doubles the value**. So for a free lead course, target **45–60 minutes, self-paced, with a short quiz and an auto-issued certificate.** Video is common but **text + quiz is explicitly acceptable** for endorsement — assessment and clear learning outcomes matter far more than production format.

### 2. REPs NZ (reps.org.nz) — VERIFIED, defines the NZ endorsement maths
- **Source:** https://reps.org.nz/cpd-provider-information/ — the CPD allocation rules (quotes):
  - *"CPD allocation is based on 5 CPD Points per 8 hours training."* (≈ 0.625 pts/hour)
  - **Assessment doubles it:** *"Courses/events that are fully assessed (i.e. have an exam and/or practical assessment at the end ... that enables a pass or fail result) receive double CPD points allocation (so 10 CPD points for 8 hours of training) for those that pass."*
  - **Short courses scale down proportionally:** *"a 4 hour course that is non assessed is 2.5 CPD points."* → a **1-hour non-assessed** course ≈ **0.5–0.6 pts**; assessed ≈ **1.25 pts**.
  - **Online/correspondence allowed:** *"Correspondence courses may be allocated CPD points as long as they are assessed, but receive CPD points allocation equivalent to a non assessed course."*
  - **Floor/ceiling:** *"A course/event may be awarded a maximum of 20 CPD points, the minimum is .5."*
- **Source:** https://reps.org.nz/ongoing-education-cpd/ — quotes:
  - Requirement: *"REPs requires 10 CPD points per year"* and *"Each year a registered individual is now required to gain 10 CPD points all of which must come from ongoing education."*
  - Endorsed courses are quality-gated: *"These REPs verified CPD courses are assessed for quality and relevance."*
  - **Format reality (from the live endorsed list):** many endorsed offerings are **online, self-paced, "Ongoing"** enrolment (e.g. Precision Nutrition, Girls Gone Strong, The BioMechanics Method modules). Point values scale with size: a small module like *"Exercise as Medicine – Prostate Cancer and Exercise Professionals — 1 CPD point"* vs multi-day certs at 20. **Short single-topic online modules (1–8 pts) are normal and endorsable.**

**Takeaway for CEA:** to be REPs-endorsable and worth points, a course needs (a) stated length, (b) an **assessment** to earn the doubled/meaningful rate, (c) provider registration + notifying run dates. Online self-paced is fine.

### 3. CASES / formerly BASES (cases.org.uk) — VERIFIED endorsement criteria
- **Source (PDF, downloaded & extracted):** https://www.cases.org.uk/imgs/cases_cpd_endorsement_scheme_guidelines___apr_25689.pdf — "CASES CPD Endorsement Guidelines and principles."
- **What endorsement actually requires** (peer-assessed against a checklist; *"not every criterion is essential ... expected to meet the majority"*). Load-bearing criteria quoted verbatim:
  - *"Are the learning outcomes clearly stated?"*
  - *"If a qualification or certificate is awarded, is a satisfactory method of assessment in place?"* (assessment required **only if** you award a cert)
  - *"Is the CPD content underpinned by a well-established scientific evidence-base?"*
  - *"Are the overall objectives of the CPD, the target audience and what is involved all clear?"*
  - Evaluation/feedback mechanism + complaints process; H&S/insurance if applicable.
- **Eligible formats explicitly include:** *"Educational modules ... Online courses ... Learning, educational or technical workshops."*
- **No minimum length is stated.** Credits are outcome-based and capped: *"no single endorsed event will be awarded more than 10 credits"* (per the endorse page https://www.cases.org.uk/spage-organisations-endorse_your_cpd_event.html). Verified example credit values (Table 1): a 2-day conference = 2 credits; short online courses = 5; an online Graduate Diploma = 10. Credits reflect quality/scope, not a rigid hours formula.
- **Endorsement is a paid B2B scheme:** initial fee £250 (HEI/sole trader/non-profit) to £500 (commercial) per course/year, plus renewal; optional 15% booking-fee admin service.

## SYNTHESIS

### A. Typical shape of a CPD short course on these sites
- **Length:** ~1 hour is the modal unit (OT Potential: *"1–1.5 hours"*, 1 CEU each). Short single-topic modules worth ~0.5–8 points are normal (REPs list). Longer certs (multi-day, 20 pts) exist but are the exception, not the lead product.
- **Format:** multi-format and increasingly **audio/text-first**. OT Potential explicitly offers *"Text-based courses with transcripts"* alongside video/podcast/webinar. Video is expected-and-nice but **not required** — text + quiz is a legitimate, endorsable format (REPs allows assessed correspondence courses; CASES lists "online courses / educational modules").
- **Assessment:** a **short quiz** is the near-universal mechanism to "earn" the credit (OT Potential on every course). Under REPs, assessment is what unlocks the meaningful/doubled point rate. Under CASES, assessment is required *if* you award a certificate.
- **Certificate:** an **instant/auto PDF certificate of completion** is standard and expected (OT Potential: *"an instant PDF certificate of completion"*).

### B. What length/format is expected / converts
- These bodies expect a **short, self-paced module (≈45–60 min)** as the standard consumable unit, **not** a multi-hour course. CEA's flagship 14-hour offering is the paid product; the lead course should be a single ~1-hour module.
- **Video is not mandatory.** A well-structured **text + short-quiz + certificate** module is acceptable for endorsement and matches the audio/text-first direction of the leading precedent. Invest effort in **clear learning outcomes and a real assessment**, not production value.

### C. Free-lead → paid conversion structure (from OT Potential, the live precedent)
1. Offer **2 fully-functional free courses** — real content, real quiz, real certificate/CEU. Do **not** cripple them into teasers; the free course must stand alone as genuinely useful and credit-worthy.
2. Gate the **library and volume** ("earn all your required CPD/CEU here"), not a better single course. The upsell is *breadth + convenience of one-stop compliance*, plus member-only extras (handouts, tools, AI chat).
3. Price the entry **very low and low-friction** ($9/mo after a small setup, or ~$99/yr; lifetime option). Reduce the decision, don't maximise first-transaction size.
4. Keep the free and paid experience **identical in shape** (same quiz+cert flow) so the free course trains the exact behaviour that converts.

### D. Genuinely REQUIRED for endorsement/listing vs nice-to-have
**Required (verified):**
- **Clearly stated learning outcomes / objectives + defined target audience** (CASES criteria; REPs quality-vetting).
- **An assessment** — quiz/exam/practical with a pass/fail — if you want a certificate (CASES) and if you want meaningful/doubled CPD points (REPs). Effectively required for a credit-bearing course.
- **Evidence base:** content *"underpinned by a well-established scientific evidence-base"* (CASES).
- **Provider process:** register as a CPD provider and notify run dates (REPs); pay the endorsement fee and pass peer-assessment (CASES £250–£500/course/yr).
- **Governance basics:** evaluation/feedback + complaints process; H&S/insurance where applicable (CASES).

**Nice-to-have / NOT required (verified):**
- **No minimum course length** is stated by CASES or REPs — points simply scale with length (REPs floor 0.5 pt) and quality (CASES).
- **Video is not required** — online/text/correspondence formats are explicitly endorsable if assessed.
- A **certificate** is optional to CASES endorsement per se, but is expected by users and, in practice, ties to the assessment requirement, so include one.
