**Description**
> This protocol standardises the assessment-led, exercise-prescription pathway for the rehabilitation of concussion / mild traumatic brain injury, reflecting the shift established by the 6th International Consensus Statement on Concussion in Sport (Amsterdam, 2022) from prolonged rest to early, individually prescribed sub-symptom-threshold aerobic exercise as first-line treatment.
>
> It specifies: (1) screening for contraindications to graded exertion; (2) administration of a standardised, symptom-limited graded exercise test — the Buffalo Concussion Treadmill Test (BCTT) or Buffalo Concussion Bike Test (BCBT) — to derive an individual heart-rate threshold at symptom exacerbation; (3) conversion of that threshold into a sub-symptom-threshold aerobic prescription (target heart-rate band, session structure, and the symptom-exacerbation stop rule); (4) progression governed by re-test data and heart-rate response rather than elapsed time, including the reduce-rather-than-rest response to a symptom flare; (5) the graded return-to-sport / return-to-activity strategy and the two governing minimums (mandated stand-down and symptom-free period); and (6) documentation and scope-of-practice boundaries — diagnosis and medical clearance are reserved to the treating medical practitioner.
>
> The protocol is the clinical spine of the Concussion Rehab Mastery (CRM) course: eight modules, 480 minutes of assessed instructional content, referenced to a 136-item peer-reviewed evidence base. It is published to provide accreditation bodies and clinicians a citable, reviewable standard of the method taught.
