# ACC supplier pricing — market comparables + per-target map (all 25)

**Compiled 2026-07-27.** Builds on the corrected model in NZ-ACC-PRICING-MODEL.md (owner decisions 2026-07-20: **NZ$49/delivering-clinician/mo** tool subscription · **seats NZ$895 → $425 at 40+** · **pilot free for first two orgs** · never model episode volume — ask in the first call).

All prices NZD excl GST. "Delivering clinicians" = clinicians actually running concussion exercise rehab, NOT headcount. **Every D estimate below is a call-validated question, not a fact — quote the per-clinician rates, let them tell you D.**

---

## 1. What the market charges (researched 2026-07-27)

| Comparable | What it is | Price | Read-across |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gensolve GPM** (their own PMS) | Practice management, per-FTE-clinician, admin free | ~NZ$79/mo (1 user) → ~$399/mo (10) → ~$799/mo (100) — i.e. ~NZ$8–79/clinician/mo by scale | Our $49 sits inside the band they already accept for per-clinician clinical software; at enterprise scale we cost more per clinician than their PMS — justified only by the funded-episode + reporting value, so lead with that |
| **Physitrack** | Exercise-prescription app clinicians already buy | £17.99/user/mo (~NZ$38); annual ~£194/user; volume discounts 100+ | The floor. We're ~30% above the generic exercise tool — defensible: measured threshold, verified sessions, ACC884 output. Don't price below it (signals "another exercise app") |
| **VALD** (ForceDecks/TeleHab) | Measurement tech NZ physio groups already lease | Software alone USD$2,900/yr (~NZ$4,800) per 3-computer site + hardware lease, 3-yr terms | Proof NZ allied-health orgs pay thousands/yr/site for *measurement*. A 6-clinician clinic on SST ($3.5k/yr) is cheaper than one VALD site licence |
| **ImPACT** | Concussion-specific baseline/assessment | Quote-gated annual packages (historically ~US$600–800/yr small clinic) | Concussion-specific tooling is normally sold annual-subscription, quote-gated — our negotiated-commercials posture is market-normal |
| **CCMI** (Complete Concussion Mgmt) | The closest analog anywhere: training course + clinic certification + software network | Course quote-gated (runs ~CAD$800-class with 50%-off promos) + recurring Academy membership + clinic certification | The bundle model (edu-for-competency + tools + network badge) is commercially proven. Nobody runs it in NZ. |
| **NZ CPD market** | Physio/OT full-day courses | ~NZ$400–900/day typical | Seats at $895 for 16 ESSA-accredited CPD + org compliance artifact = top of market but inside it; $425 enterprise seat is comfortably under it |

**Verdict: the standing card is reasonable.** $49/clinician/mo ≈ **NZ$11.30/clinician/week ≈ 4 minutes of TBI21 billing** ($155.27/hr) — it pays for itself if it saves five minutes a week, and the ACC884 compile saves hours per episode of *unbillable* report time. That's the line for every call.

## 2b. Education access tiers — online AND on-site (added 2026-07-27)

The seat ladder above prices ONLINE access only (8 CPD, per-clinician certificate).
The practical layer for NZ orgs is delivered ON-SITE — their teams cannot attend AU
practical days, so the day travels to them. This mirrors the AU complete tier:
online seat + on-site day ≈ NZ$1,350/clinician all-in, right beside the AU
complete price (A$1,190–1,400) — coherent, defensible, no new price logic.

| Tier | What | Price *(proposed)* |
|---|---|---|
| **Online seat** | CCM or CRM online, 8 CPD, certificate per clinician | $895 → $425 ladder (§2) |
| **Private on-site practical day** | Zac delivers the CCM practical day at THEIR clinic — graded exercise testing, VOMS/BESS, cervical/vestibular; hands-on, equipment-free (the ESSA-amended format). Up to 12 clinicians | **NZ$5,500/day** (+$395/head 13–16; $5,000/day at 3+ days) + travel at cost |
| **Shared regional day** | Open workshop day (Christchurch, New Plymouth, Tauranga…) for small orgs — per-head | **NZ$495/head** |

Why on-site beats sending staff out (the selling line): a clinic sending 10 clinicians
to an external course loses 10 billable days plus travel; the on-site day costs
$5,500 flat, standardises the whole team at once, and the org keeps the certificates
in its Cl. 15.2 quality file. **Never book rival orgs into the same private day**
(Cl. 13.4 sensitivity); the shared regional day is for non-competing smalls or
mixed disciplines.

## 2c. Why they buy — per product (the rationale that survives procurement)

| Product | The buyer's reason, in their terms |
|---|---|
| **SST Trainer** | The contract obliges exercise-tolerance assessment (Cl. 5.8.2.1.2) that no mandated discipline is trained at entry to deliver (5.2.1). Every hour writing ACC884s is unbillable (5.10/5.13.6) — SST compiles it as care happens. Their recovery-duration numbers are shared non-anonymised with competitors (13.4) and the 2028 extension is performance-conditional (1.2.2): a measured HRt trajectory is the defensible evidence. Between-visit telemetry means funded hours aren't spent asking "how did the week go?" |
| **Baseline** | "Entered Stage 1 within two weeks" is a scored measure — baseline-engaged athletes present early. One club link = a referral front door with zero appointment cost: every baseline athlete who later presents is a funded episode (~$2,265 blended) they'd otherwise never see. Club relationships compound. |
| **Education (online seats)** | Documented competency for the Cl. 15.2 quality file — per-clinician, accredited (OA + ESSA PDNF26077), auditable. Their staff have CPD requirements and budgets anyway; this points existing spend at the contract's weakest competency. |
| **On-site practical day** | Graded exercise testing is a hands-on skill — video alone doesn't make a team consistent across sites. On-site costs less than the lost billable days of sending staff away, and standardises everyone in one pass. |
| **The package logic** | Instruments without competency produce reports a clinician can't defend; competency without instruments produces trained clinicians who still can't see the treatment. One clinic code, filed into Gensolve. |

## 4. Rules for the calls

1. **Quote rates, not totals.** "$49 per delivering clinician per month, seats from $425" — then ask D. Never state their episode volume or revenue.
2. **The Gensolve anchor:** they already pay per-clinician monthly for GPM. This is one more line on a bill format they already accept — and it files into GPM.
3. **The unbillable-time line:** report time can't be invoiced (Cl. 5.10/5.13.6). Tool cost ≈ 4 min of TBI21/week; ACC884 compile returns hours of unbillable time per episode.
4. **Gensolve write gate = the pilot offer,** never a caveat discovered later: "switching writes on for your tenant is part of the pilot."
5. **Pilot slots (2, free):** spend them on reference value — Tui (design partner) + one of Rehab Taranaki (T3 warm) or Hemisphere (fast close). NOT on Tier 1; Active+/Habit pay.
6. **LFBIT:** charity — if it stalls on price, hold rates and gift seats, don't discount the tool.
7. Who pays — supplier margin or recoverable within the funded service — is still THE first-call question.
