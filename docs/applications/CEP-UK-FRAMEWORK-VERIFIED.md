## CLAIM 1 — "six practice areas and nine conditions" — VERIFIED

**Source:** https://www.clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk/cpd-opportunities (fetched 2026-07-21, HTTP 200)

> "To gain and maintain AHCS registration as a Clinical Exercise Physiologist we must be proficient across **six areas of practice for nine health conditions**."

### The exact SIX areas of practice
From **"Clinical Exercise Physiology Curriculum Framework"** (CEP-UK), the six knowledge-and-skills domains, in order:
1. **Pathophysiology and Clinical Management**
2. **Screening and Risk Stratification**
3. **Assessment of Health Status and Functional Capacity**
4. **Design of Exercise Interventions**
5. **Exercise Delivery and Implementation**
6. **Behaviour Change and Communication**

**Source PDF:** https://www.clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk/_files/ugd/92398c_772ac36da0c44e629e5bed13d60b9cc5.pdf (title line: "Clinical Exercise Physiology Curriculum Framework"). Each of the six appears as a section header immediately followed by "Knowledge and skills".

*Note on exact phrasing:* the phrase "six areas of practice" is the CPD-page's own label; the six named domains come from the Curriculum Framework. The Evidence-of-Clinical-Practice form (below) cross-references "CEP Scope of Practice – Criterion 5a…5f" — six sub-criteria under Criterion 5, consistent with six areas. The CPD-opportunities page also carries a 6×9 grid as an embedded image (not machine-readable); the six domain labels above are the authoritative text source.

### The exact NINE health conditions
From **"The Scope of Practice for a UK Clinical Exercise Physiologist"** (CEP-UK):
1. **Cancer**
2. **Cardiovascular**
3. **Frailty**
4. **Renal** (the portfolio Evidence form labels this **"Kidney"**)
5. **Mental Health**
6. **Metabolic**
7. **Musculoskeletal**
8. **Neurological/Neuromuscular** (the portfolio Evidence form labels this **"Neurological"**)
9. **Respiratory/Pulmonary** (the portfolio Evidence form labels this **"Respiratory"**)

**Source PDF (Scope of Practice):** https://www.clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk/_files/ugd/92398c_da6616d13dae4ea2ba15c0ccd18edbc6.pdf

> "n Cancer … n Cardiovascular … n Frailty e.g. sarcopenia, osteoporosis, falls history or fear of falling. n Renal e.g. chronic kidney disease (CKD) stages 1-5 … n Mental Health … n Metabolic … n Musculoskeletal … n **Neurological/Neuromuscular e.g. stroke, dementia, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis.** n Respiratory/Pulmonary e.g. asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), cystic fibrosis."

**Corroborating source (portfolio Evidence form):** https://documents.ahcs.ac.uk/docs/100/-110-Clinical-Exercise-Physiologist-Evidence-of-Clinical-Practice-v2.0-September-2023.pdf — lists exactly nine "Health Condition" rows with nine Yes/No fields: "Cancer, Cardiovascular, Frailty, Kidney, Mental Health, Metabolic, Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Respiratory."

**Verdict:** VERIFIED. Six areas × nine conditions is the real registration framework. Exact labels above.

## CLAIM 2 — Neurological in scope (does concussion map at all?) — VERIFIED, with a caveat

**Neurological IS one of the nine conditions** (see Claim 1). The Scope of Practice states it as **"Neurological/Neuromuscular e.g. stroke, dementia, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis."**

**Caveat that must be handled honestly in outreach:**
- In the framework, **"Neurological/Neuromuscular" is a CONDITION, not a practice area.** The pack's mapping table currently labels the "Practice area evidenced" column "Neurological" — that is a category error against the framework and has been corrected in the pack.
- **Concussion / mTBI is NOT a named exemplar** in the framework's neuro list. It maps into the Neurological/Neuromuscular condition by clinical extension. The pack should not imply concussion is called out by CEP-UK; it should present CRM as evidence *within* the Neurological/Neuromuscular condition and across the relevant practice areas.

**Verdict:** Target is valid on the mapping axis — neuro is in the grid — but the honest framing is "evidence within the Neurological/Neuromuscular condition," not "concussion is a named condition."

## CLAIM 4 — "up to £650 CPD funding per individual" — REAL FIGURE, PACK MISFRAMES IT

**Source:** https://www.clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk/cpd-opportunities (fetched 2026-07-21, HTTP 200)

> "The CASES and Clinical Exercise Physiology UK CPD grant has been established to provide annual opportunities for **four** BASES professional / graduate members. **Up to £650 of funding will be provided** to meet the CPD requirements that individuals might need to become an Academy of Healthcare Sciences (AHCS) registered Clinical Exercise Physiologist (CEP) or to maintain registration. **Application closing date: 31st March 2024.**"

**What this actually is:** a **competitive grant, four awards per year, up to £650 each, with an annual application deadline** (the visible cycle closed 31 March 2024). It is administered by CASES/CEP-UK, tied to BASES membership.

**What the pack currently (wrongly) asserts:**
- "Each registrant has **up to £650 of funding** available" (READ THIS FIRST)
- "**Funded (£650 each)**" (internal notes)
- "and **up to £650** to spend closing whatever is still open" (Email 2)

**This is an overclaim.** There is **no evidence** that each of the 164 registrants holds a £650 CPD budget. The "funded buyer list" pillar of the pack's thesis is **not supported by the source**. Corrected in the pack; the funding line is now stated as the competitive CASES/CEP-UK grant it actually is.

**Verdict:** The £650 number is real and sourced — but the per-individual entitlement framing is UNSUPPORTED and has been corrected.

## CLAIM 5 — portfolio route closes 31 December 2026 — VERIFIED (re-confirmed)

**Source:** https://www.ahcs.ac.uk/2025/12/10/cep-uk-and-ahcs-to-close-portfolio-registration-pathway-for-clinical-exercise-physiologists-in-december-2026/

> "CEP-UK and the Academy for Healthcare Science (AHCS) have announced that the **portfolio registration pathway (also referred to as the equivalence pathway)** for Clinical Exercise Physiologists (CEPs) **will close on 31st December 2026.** After this date, registration with the AHCS will only be available to individuals who have graduated from AHCS-accredited MSc Clinical Exercise Physiology programmes."

**What closes:** the **portfolio / equivalence** entry route — not CEP registration itself (the MSc-accredited route continues). Applicants with an application in by 31 Dec 2026 "will be given sufficient time to complete the registration process."

**Verdict:** VERIFIED. Date and scope both confirmed verbatim, matching the matrix's prior VERIFIED entry.

## BONUS FINDING — the domain error

**`cep.uk.com` is NOT the CEP-UK register.** Live SSL handshake fails; the closest Wayback snapshot (2018) is a Flash-based site for an engineering/precision-tools/facilities company ("d tools", "form tools", "Facilities", "100KTO1"). CDX for `cep.uk.com*` returns only that company's assets.

The **real CEP-UK site is `www.clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk`** (confirmed by the AHCS portfolio Evidence form, which directs applicants there: "the Application Guidance document provided on the CEP-UK website … https://www.clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk/application-resources").

Any target-list or pack reference to cep.uk.com as the register should be replaced with clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk (front-of-house) and ahcs.ac.uk (the register operator).
