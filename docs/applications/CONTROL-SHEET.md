## Course accreditations / listings

### 1. ACSM (USA) — CRM
- **What:** application to become an ACSM Approved Education Provider, so CRM can award ACSM CECs.
- **Where it goes:** online form at `acsm.formstack.com/forms/acsm_education_provider_application_2024`. Enquiries `education@acsm.org`.
- **How to apply:** fill the Formstack form, paste the pack's asset copy (A1–A9 are written), pay the fee the form reveals, submit. Review is about 4 weeks.
- **Fee:** not published — revealed inside the form. Estimate USD $300–600.
- **Only-you:** AHPRA number (Part A #1). Everything else in the pack is written.
- **Note:** `/acsm` landing page is already live (they require it before approval).

### 2. CEP-UK / AHCS (UK) — CRM
- **What:** NOT an accreditation. Targeted outreach to a known group of clinical exercise physiologists registering via the portfolio route, proposing CRM as recognised CPD.
- **Where it goes:** the AHCS / `clinicalexercisephysiology.org.uk` CPD contact. **Note: `cep.uk.com` is a defunct unrelated site — do not use it.** Register is at `ahcs.ac.uk`.
- **How to apply:** send the two emails in the pack — one to AHCS proposing recognition, one to registrants. There is no form.
- **The clock:** the portfolio route **closes 31 December 2026.** This is the deadline that makes CEP-UK the priority target.
- **Only-you:** AHPRA number; GBP price; live course URL. **AND** the lawful basis + source for the registrant contact list (UK GDPR/PECR — you cannot cold-email a list you don't have a lawful basis for). Verified: neuro is one of the nine conditions, concussion maps in by extension.

### 3. CASES (UK) — CRM
- **What:** CPD Endorsement application. Gets CASES credits logo + their newsletter + quarterly magazine + social promotion.
- **Where it goes:** `education@cases.org.uk`.
- **How to apply:** email for the current fee, then submit the application content in the pack. ~2 week review.
- **Fee:** estimate £300–600; rejected applications refunded minus £50. **Do NOT budget from the £1,800 figure on their site — that is degree endorsement, not CPD.**
- **Only-you:** AHPRA number; GBP price.

### 4. CIMSPA (UK) — free course as the endorsed product
- **What:** Training Provider Partnership + product endorsement. The free course is the endorsed product; CRM is the upsell.
- **Where it goes:** `trainingpartners@cimspa.co.uk` **and** `info@cimspa.co.uk` (their partner page has a service fault redirecting to info@).
- **How to apply:** email to confirm the next submission **window** BEFORE paying anything. Endorsement runs in bi-monthly windows, not rolling.
- **Fee:** £140+VAT admin (pay before review) + £280+VAT annual licence + £280+VAT per product = ~£700+VAT year one.
- **Only-you:** AHPRA number; GBP price. **Lowest priority — manufactured demand. Send the window enquiry, spend nothing until it replies.**

### 5. CSEP (Canada) — CRM
- **What:** Route 2 formal accreditation to promote CRM to CSEP-certified members.
- **Where it goes:** `info@csep.ca`.
- **How to apply:** email for the Route 2 process + fee. **Route 1 (member self-report) is free and works TODAY — a CSEP member can already claim CRM as PDCs without you doing anything.**
- **Fee:** estimate CAD $0–500.
- **Only-you:** AHPRA number; CAD price. **Lowest priority — CCMI already serves Canada. Fire Route 2 the day ESSA lands (CSEP recognises ESSA equivalency).**

### 6. SESNZ / CEPNZ (NZ) — free course / CRM listing
- **What:** courteous request to list on the SESNZ CPD Opportunities page. Not a formal application.
- **Where it goes:** `natmanager@sesnz.org.nz`, CC CEPNZ.
- **How to apply:** send the short listing email in the pack.
- **Fee:** likely free.
- **Only-you:** NZD price; live course URL; CEPNZ CC address.

### 7. South Africa / HPCSA — CRM
- **What:** per-activity CEU accreditation via an approved accreditor (BASA or UCT). NOT a provider scheme — you accredit the *activity*.
- **Where it goes:** BASA `basa@biokineticssa.org.za` (Phumzile Tjiane), or the UCT CPD office.
- **How to apply:** email the accreditor to confirm fee + process, submit the per-activity application in the pack.
- **Fee:** benchmark R1,380 (short course) up to R2,750. **Non-refundable even if declined** — so decide the ZAR price first.
- **HARD RULES:** accreditation must PRECEDE any SA enrolment (no retrospective CEUs, HPCSA rule since 1 Nov 2024). And the fee is charged even if withdrawn/declined.
- **Only-you (this one has real work beyond Part A):**
  1. **ZAR price.**
  2. **Has any SA practitioner already enrolled in CRM?** If yes, disclose it — those completions can't earn CEUs.
  3. **Write the SA annex** (a short document the pack references but that doesn't exist yet).
  4. **Build the ZA enrolment block** (geo-gate CRM so no SA practitioner can enrol before accreditation lands — otherwise Rule 1 is breached).
