## 1. WHAT THIS CHANNEL IS

ACC contracts a defined set of supplier organisations to deliver **Concussion Services** — a staged, interdisciplinary service for people at risk of delayed recovery from mild-to-moderate TBI. It is a closed, contracted market with a published supplier list, mandated team composition, mandated six-monthly reporting and explicit performance benchmarking against the national average.

That combination is what makes it a procurement conversation rather than a CPD conversation.

**Market size:**
- ~21,000 new concussion-related ACC claims per year (2016–2021 average) — [bpacnz](https://bpac.org.nz/2022/concussion.aspx)
- Eligibility is narrower than the claim count: the client must have been injured within the last 12 months and have "a risk of delayed recovery" (Service Schedule 4.1.2.5, 4.1.2.7)
- At a plausible 15–25% referral rate that is roughly **3,000–5,000 funded service episodes per year** *(founder estimate — not an ACC-published figure)*
- 25 organisations share that volume

## 2. THE TIMING HOOK — CORRECTED

**The founder brief carried "1+1+1, suppliers are at the year-one renewal point now." The primary sources do not fully support that, and the real hook is sharper.**

What each source actually says:

| Source | Wording |
|---|---|
| GETS Advance Notice ([id=31320863](https://www.gets.govt.nz/ACC/ExternalTenderDetails.htm?id=31320863)) | "1 year with 2 further rights of renewal of 1 year (i.e., 1+1+1 year)" from 1 July 2025 |
| **Service Schedule, effective 01 July 2026** ([PDF](https://www.acc.co.nz/assets/contracts/concussion-services-service-schedule.pdf)) | "The Term ... is the period from **1 July 2025** (Start date) until the close of **30 June 2027** (End date)" — then "the Parties may agree in writing to extend the Term ... for **one further term of one year**" |

So the *executed* schedule is a **two-year initial term to 30 June 2027, plus one one-year extension** — not 1+1+1. **There is no renewal decision on the table this month.** Do not open with "you're at your renewal point" — a National Manager will know that is wrong, and it burns the first email.

**The real hook is better, and it is live right now.** Two clauses do the work:

1. **The extension is performance-conditional.** Clause 1.2.2: extension depends on "**ACC being satisfied with the performance of the Services by the Supplier.**" Performance recorded across 2026–27 is what buys the extension to June 2028.

2. **The six-monthly report for 1 Jan – 30 June 2026 is due right now** — within **ten business days from 30 June** (Clause 13.3), submitted to `ConcussionServices@acc.co.nz` ([Operational Guidelines](https://www.acc.co.nz/assets/contracts/concussion-og.pdf)). Every supplier is looking at its own numbers this fortnight.

3. **And those numbers get shared with their competitors.** Clause 13.4: "The Supplier consents to ACC sharing non-anonymised ACC information in its reports, including information relating to the Supplier's performance under this Contract, **with all other contracted suppliers** providing Concussion Services to ACC."

That is the opening: *you have just filed the numbers, your competitors will see them, and the extension to 2028 turns on them.*

## 3. WHAT SUPPLIERS ARE MEASURED ON

From Service Schedule Part B, Table 4 — every measure is benchmarked as "**National Average or one deviation higher**", sourced from ACC data (not self-report):

| Measure | Description |
|---|---|
| Service progression | Proportion of clients who enter Stage 2 (Treatment & Rehab) from Stage 1 (Education & Assessment) |
| Timeliness of service entry | Proportion entering Stage 1 **within two weeks** of injury date |
| Timeliness of service entry | Proportion entering Stage 1 **after six months** of injury date |
| Return to work — effectiveness | Proportion receiving weekly compensation **more than 26 weeks** after entering Stage 1 |
| Return to work — efficiency | Average number of weeks of weekly compensation after entering Stage 1 |
| Return to work — sustainability | Proportion who **sustained return to work for at least three months** |

Clause 12.3.1 puts suppliers at risk where performance "differs materially from that of comparable suppliers in the absence of a clear difference in the client groups."

**Every one of these is a recovery-duration metric.** Nothing here rewards assessment volume. The entire scorecard rewards getting people back to work faster and keeping them there — which is exactly what a heart-rate-threshold exercise prescription is for.

## 4. THE STRUCTURAL GAP — THE ARGUMENT THAT CARRIES THIS CHANNEL

ACC mandates the team. Service Schedule 5.2.1: the supplier "must maintain a core Interdisciplinary Team in each Service location which consists of a minimum of a: **Medical Practitioner. Neuropsychologist. Clinical psychologists and/or Psychologists. Occupational therapist. Physiotherapist.**"

Non-core consult list (5.2.2): registered nurses, speech language therapists, social workers.

**No exercise physiologist. No exercise-testing competency anywhere in the mandated team.**

Yet the schedule obliges the supplier to deliver, at 5.8.2.1.2, "**Assessment of exercise tolerance and/or functional capacity for undertaking programmes**" — and since Amsterdam 2022 the guideline first-line treatment for delayed recovery is sub-symptom-threshold aerobic exercise prescribed from an **individually measured** heart-rate threshold.

The obligation exists. The competency to discharge it is not inside the mandated team as constituted. That gap is solvable with training rather than a new hire — and it lands directly on the three return-to-work measures the supplier is benchmarked on.

**That is the whole pitch. Lead with it. Everything else is supporting material.**

## 7. APPROACH BY TIER

### The universal rule: the ACC emails are intake queues, not doors

Every email on ACC's list is a *referral* address — clinical intake. A capability pitch sent there dies. Its only legitimate use is a two-line request for a name.

**The role you actually want exists and is named in the contract.** Service Schedule 5.1: "The Supplier will nominate a person as their **Relationship manager** to be the main contact with ACC for all operational issues relating to the provision of this Service." And Part A, Table 5 sets the supplier-side counterpart to ACC's Engagement and Performance Manager as the "**Operational contact / National Manager**."

So the ask is precise and verifiable, and it signals you have read the contract:

> "Who is your nominated Relationship Manager for the ACC Concussion Services contract?"

That single sentence separates CEA from every generic CPD vendor that ever emailed them.

---

### TIER 1 — Large multi-region (Active+, Habit, Proactive/ABI, APM, TBI Health, Geneva, Axis)

**Address:** the National Manager / Head of Clinical Excellence / Head of Service Delivery — **not** the CEO, and never the referrals inbox. Named starting points are listed per-organisation above (Dr Pauline Penny at Active+; Emma Davidson at APM; Shane Rossiter at Geneva; Mark Shirley or Lauren Kilkolly at Habit; Michelle Wilkinson across Proactive/ABI; Dr Dan Exeter at Axis).

**Frame:** procurement and performance, not education. These organisations have a training budget and an L&D function; they do not have a solution to the exercise-testing gap.

**The opening argument, in order:**
1. Their six-monthly report for 1 Jan–30 June 2026 has just gone in, and Clause 13.4 means their competitors will see it.
2. Their extension past 30 June 2027 is conditional on ACC being satisfied with performance (Clause 1.2.2).
3. Every performance measure is a recovery-duration metric benchmarked against the national average.
4. ACC mandates a five-discipline team with no exercise-testing competency, while obliging them to assess exercise tolerance (5.8.2.1.2) and while the guideline first-line treatment is an individually measured HR-threshold prescription.
5. That gap is closable by training the physiotherapists they already employ.

**Deal shape:** team licence / supplier-wide rollout. Do not quote per-seat retail. Do not lead with CPD hours.

**Expect a long cycle.** These are procurement-governed organisations. Anchor on Active+ for the size of the prize, but **do not gate anything else on it** — see the parallel-track note below.

---

### TIER 2 — Mid-size regional

**Address:** the Director, Clinical Lead or owner directly. In organisations this size that person is usually reachable and usually decides.

**Frame:** competitive differentiation against the nationals. A Tier 2 supplier is benchmarked against Active+ and Habit on the same ACC scorecard, with a fraction of the resource. An objective, defensible exercise-threshold protocol is the cheapest available way to out-perform a national on return-to-work duration in their own region.

**Deal shape:** small team licence, 3–15 clinicians. Fast decision, real money, and — critically — **a live reference**.

---

### TIER 3 — Single-clinic / single-region specialists

**Address:** the owner-clinician. One person, one email, no procurement.

**Frame:** peer-to-peer, founder to founder. Zac is an osteopath who built this himself; a single-clinic owner responds to that and not to a capability deck. Keep it short and specific.

**Deal shape:** individual or micro-team. Low revenue — **these are not a revenue play, they are the reference-generation play.**
