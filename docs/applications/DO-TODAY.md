## The NZ strategy in three lines

1. **Associations sell the course.** No NZ regulator accredits courses — PBNZ and OTBNZ both run self-directed CPD with audit. So "endorsement" means association reach and trust, not a stamp.
2. **The 25 ACC suppliers buy SST subscriptions.** Different motion, different buyer.
3. **The association channel is top-of-funnel for the supplier sale.** A physio at Active+ who sat in a SEPNZ webinar is a warm route into Active+. Run them in that order, not in parallel isolation.

**Match product to channel — this is the mistake to avoid:**

| Channel | Send | Never send |
|---|---|---|
| SEPNZ, PNZ Neurology SIG, OTNZ-WNA | **CCM** (clinical) | the free short course |
| REPS NZ, SESNZ/CEPNZ | **free/entry course** | CCM — REPS registrants are largely PT-level and mostly can't use it |

Putting CCM through REPS burns an endorsement fee on an audience that can't buy it. Putting the free course through SEPNZ undersells the audience that can.
