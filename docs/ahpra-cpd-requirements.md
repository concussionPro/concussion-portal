## 1. Medical Board of Australia

- **Total**: **50 hours per year** of CPD relevant to scope of practice. ([Medical Board CPD FAQ](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Policies/FAQ/FAQ-general-registration-CPD.aspx))
- **Categories** (three mandatory):
  - **Educational Activities (EA)** — min **12.5 hrs**. Reading, lectures, conferences, podcasts.
  - **Reviewing Performance (RP)** + **Measuring Outcomes (MO)** — min **25 hrs combined**, with **min 5 hrs in each**.
  - **Free choice** across the three — **12.5 hrs**.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: EA covers passive/self-directed (reading, podcasts). RP needs feedback on the practitioner's own work; MO needs data on the practitioner's own outcomes — these are not passive. So the effective "passive/self-directed" ceiling is ~37.5 hrs (12.5 EA + 12.5 free + non-interactive portions of RP/MO).
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading guidelines, journals, podcasts during the working day are explicitly EA-eligible, provided documented with reflection. Clinical practice itself is NOT CPD.
- **Recording**: All doctors must hold a **Professional Development Plan (PDP)** and log activities with their **CPD Home**. Retain evidence per CPD Home rules (commonly 3+ years).
- **Audit**: AHPRA audits CPD Homes (via Australian Medical Council). CPD Homes audit individual members. ([Medical Board CPD overview](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Professional-Performance-Framework/CPD.aspx))
- **Official URL**: [https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Policies/FAQ/FAQ-general-registration-CPD.aspx](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Policies/FAQ/FAQ-general-registration-CPD.aspx)
- Registration Standard (PDF): [ahpra.gov.au document WD21/31046](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD21/31046&dbid=AP&chksum=TqPI98CYQYllvPkGwiAz/w%3D%3D)

## 2. Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA)

- **Total**: **20 hrs/yr** for each registration held. Dual registered (nurse + midwife) = **40 hrs/yr**. Each endorsement (Nurse Practitioner, Endorsed Midwife, scheduled medicines) = **+10 hrs/yr**.
- **Categories**: None mandated. CPD must be relevant to context of practice. "1 hour of active learning = 1 hour of CPD."
- **Self-directed vs formal**: Fully self-directed permitted; no minimum interactive or formal hours.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading journals, reviewing guidelines, watching webinars all count provided practitioner can demonstrate active learning and reflection (not just passive consumption).
- **Recording**: Written documentation of all 20 hrs; reflection encouraged. **Retain 5 years**.
- **Audit**: Random annual audit (~5% of registrants); evidence required within 28 days.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/FAQ/CPD-FAQ-for-nurses-and-midwives.aspx](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/FAQ/CPD-FAQ-for-nurses-and-midwives.aspx)

## 3. Physiotherapy Board of Australia

- **Total**: **20 hrs/yr**. Pro rata = 5 hrs per 3 months of registration if registering part-way.
- **Categories**: None prescribed — Board takes a "broad view"; expects a "mix" of activity types, relevant to area of practice.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: No interactive minimum, no formal minimum. Self-directed permitted.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading clinical guidelines, journals, online modules are recognised. Not clinical practice itself.
- **Recording**: CPD portfolio retained **5 years**; produced on audit.
- **Audit**: Random audit at renewal.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.physiotherapyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines/CPD-guidelines.aspx](https://www.physiotherapyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines/CPD-guidelines.aspx)
- Registration Standard (PDF): [physiotherapyboard.gov.au WD10/207](https://www.physiotherapyboard.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD10/207&dbid=AP&chksum=Wa9V8DbNVw1XKqRGhRQZ+g%3D%3D)

## 4. Osteopathy Board of Australia

- **Total**: **25 hrs/yr** including **min 4 hrs in mandatory topics** specified annually by the Board.
- **Categories**: Mandatory topics + open choice. **First-aid certificate (Senior First Aid equivalent, updated every 3 years) is standalone** and does NOT count toward the 25 hrs.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: No formal/interactive minimum.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Recognised — reading, online learning, journal review qualify provided relevant to scope.
- **Recording**: Portfolio with learning goals and reflection. Retain **5 years**.
- **Audit**: Random audit; portfolio requested.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.osteopathyboard.gov.au/Registration-Standards/CPD-resources.aspx](https://www.osteopathyboard.gov.au/Registration-Standards/CPD-resources.aspx)

## 5. Chiropractic Board of Australia

- **Total**: **25 hrs/yr** (pro rata available if practising <9 months).
- **Categories**: Revised Dec 2019 — **"formal/informal" distinction removed**. Must include 4 hrs of Board-set mandatory topics. Activities must "improve patient outcomes, safety and experience" and draw on best available evidence.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: No formal minimum since the 2019 revision.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading and self-directed learning explicitly allowed.
- **First-aid**: Maintain HLTAID001 (CPR) or HLTAID003 (First Aid) qualification.
- **Recording**: Maintain a record for **at least 5 years**.
- **Audit**: Random audit.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.chiropracticboard.gov.au/Codes-guidelines/FAQ/Continuing-professional-development/CPD-activities-that-meet-the-standard.aspx](https://www.chiropracticboard.gov.au/Codes-guidelines/FAQ/Continuing-professional-development/CPD-activities-that-meet-the-standard.aspx)

## 6. Psychology Board of Australia

- **Total**: **30 hrs/yr** for general registration, full year.
- **Categories**:
  - **General CPD**: 20 hrs (broad — courses, reading, workshops).
  - **Peer Consultation**: **minimum 10 hrs** — distinguishing feature of Psychology Board.
  - **Endorsement-specific**: practitioners with area of practice endorsement must complete CPD specific to that area.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: Up to 20 hrs can be self-directed; **10 hrs MUST be peer consultation** (interactive with another psychologist). Pro rata = 2.5 hrs/month (50 min peer + 100 min general).
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading journals, research papers, watching webinars allowed in the 20 hr general bucket. Peer consultation cannot be passive.
- **Recording**: CPD log with peer consultation evidence (signed records).
- **Audit**: Annual random audit; **~10% of registrants** — highest known rate among AHPRA Boards.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Continuing-Professional-Development.aspx](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Continuing-Professional-Development.aspx)

## 7. Dental Board of Australia

- **Total**: **60 hrs over a 3-year CPD cycle** (covers 3 registration periods). Encouraged to spread ~20 hrs/yr.
- **Categories**:
  - **Clinical/scientific**: minimum **48 hrs** (80%).
  - **Non-scientific**: maximum **12 hrs** (20%).
  - No formal sub-categories.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: Self-directed permitted within the 80/20 split.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading journals/guidelines allowed within clinical/scientific bucket.
- **Recording**: Excess hours **cannot be rolled over** into next 3-yr cycle. Pro rata for new registrants.
- **Applies to**: All dentists, specialists, hygienists, therapists, oral health therapists, prosthetists.
- **Audit**: Random audit at renewal.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.dentalboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines/CPD.aspx](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines/CPD.aspx)

## 8. Pharmacy Board of Australia

- **Total**: **40 CPD credits** per CPD year (1 Oct – 30 Sep). Credit-weighted, NOT pure hours.
- **Categories** (credits per hour of activity):
  - **Group 1** — knowledge acquisition (reading, listening): **1 credit/hr**. **Capped at ≤50% = max 20 credits**.
  - **Group 2** — knowledge with assessment: **2 credits/hr**.
  - **Group 3** — practice improvement / outcomes: **3 credits/hr**.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: At least **20 credits must come from Group 2 and/or Group 3** (i.e. assessed/applied — semi-formal). Group 1 (pure passive) capped at half.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Yes — Group 1 explicitly covers reading and listening. No assessment required; only a log entry.
- **Recording**: Self-record on Pharmacy Board CPD plan; retain for 5 years.
- **Audit**: Random audit.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.pharmacyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines/FAQ/CPD-FAQ.aspx](https://www.pharmacyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines/FAQ/CPD-FAQ.aspx)
- Guidelines (PDF): [pharmacyboard.gov.au WD15/19249](https://www.pharmacyboard.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD15/19249&dbid=AP&chksum=6BE/hTsOAkCXQSx58VB4Xw%3D%3D)

## 9. Optometry Board of Australia

  - **Non-therapeutic**: **20 hrs/yr**.

- **Recording**: Mandatory **CPD portfolio + Learning Plan**. Retain evidence **5 years**.

## 15. Paramedicine Board of Australia

## A. RACGP CPD Home (Royal Australian College of General Practitioners)

- **Total**: **50 hrs per calendar year** — meets Medical Board standard.
- **Exact categories with minimums**:
  - **Educational Activities (EA)** — minimum **12.5 hrs**. Examples: reading, lectures, podcasts, webinars, conferences.
  - **Reviewing Performance (RP)** — minimum **5 hrs**. Reflection on feedback about the GP's work (peer review, supervision feedback, patient feedback analysis).
  - **Measuring Outcomes (MO)** — minimum **5 hrs**. Using the GP's own work data to ensure quality (mini-audits, PDSA cycles, clinical guideline development).
  - **Combined RP + MO** — minimum **25 hrs**.
  - **Flex** — **12.5 hrs** allocated to any category.
- **Program-level required activities each year** (count toward the 50 hrs):
  - At least one CPD activity addressing **culturally safe practice**.
  - At least one addressing **health inequities**.
  - At least one addressing **professionalism and ethical practice**.
  - **CPR** course once per **triennium**.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: EA is explicitly passive/self-directed (reading, podcasts). MO requires the GP's own data. RP requires feedback on the GP's own work.
- **Passive workflow activity**: AJGP reading, RACGP webinar viewing, guideline reading explicitly count as EA.
- **Recording**: Evidence retained **3 years**.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.racgp.org.au/education/professional-development/cpd/2023-triennium](https://www.racgp.org.au/education/professional-development/cpd/2023-triennium)

## B. ACRRM CPD Home (Australian College of Rural and Remote Medicine)

- **Total**: **50 hrs per year** — meets Medical Board standard.
- **Exact categories with minimums**:
  - **Educational Activities** — minimum **12.5 hrs**. Reading, courses (online/face-to-face), formal qualifications, supervised practice, lectures, workshops.
  - **Performance Review** — minimum **5 hrs**. Peer/patient feedback, teaching, supervision, practice accreditation activities.
  - **Outcome Measurement** — minimum **5 hrs**. Practice data review, case discussions, medical record reviews, clinical audits, M&M meetings.
  - **Flex** — **12.5 hrs** across any of the three categories.
  - Combined PR + OM ≥ 25 hrs (mirrors RACGP and MBA).
- **Mandatory program-level activities** (each year): culturally safe practice, health inequities, professionalism, ethical practice.
- **Specialist Fellow requirements**: **Advanced Life Support (ALS) every 3 years**. Optional MOPS (Maintenance of Professional Standards) program for procedural/mental health/emergency advanced skills — NOT mandatory.
- **Self-directed vs formal**: Educational Activities explicitly include reading and self-directed learning. PR/OM need feedback or data.
- **Passive workflow activity**: Reading and workplace lectures qualify as Educational Activities.
- **Recording**: Per CPD Home — log via ACRRM portal.
- **Audit**: ACRRM audits Home members; AMC audits ACRRM as CPD Home.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.acrrm.org.au/CPDHome/cpd-framework](https://www.acrrm.org.au/CPDHome/cpd-framework)
- 2023–2025 Handbook (PDF): [mycollege.acrrm.org.au/.../cpd-handbook-2023-2025.pdf](https://mycollege.acrrm.org.au/docs/default-source/documents/guides/cpd-handbook-2023-2025.pdf?sfvrsn=95595cec_26)

## AHPRA-wide audit posture (cross-Board summary)

- General rate: **~5% of registrants per Board per year** is the commonly cited figure. Psychology audits at a higher rate (~10%).
- Selection: **Risk-based, not purely random** — factors include prior non-compliance, complaint history, practice type/risk, and time since last audit. ([AHPRA Audit page](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Audit.aspx))
- Response time: **28 days** to provide evidence.
- Evidence retention: **5 years** is the universal rule across Boards (RACGP requires 3 years for member-level recording).
- Documentation expected: portfolio entries, certificates of attendance, reflection notes, learning plans, and (where applicable) signed peer-consultation logs.

## Implications for the CEA CPD Platform

### Boards where passive workflow activity has high CPD value (HIGH platform leverage)

These Boards permit large self-directed/reading allocations with light evidentiary burden. The platform's auto-categorisation and reflection-capture features unlock real hours here:

1. **Nursing & Midwifery (NMBA)** — 100% self-directable. "1 hr of active learning = 1 hr of CPD." Reading journals + reflection note = full credit.
2. **Physiotherapy** — broad view, no prescribed categories. Self-directed reading goes the full 20 hrs.
3. **Chiropractic** — formal/informal distinction abolished Dec 2019. Self-directed permitted up to 21 hrs (less the 4 mandatory-topic hrs).
4. **Osteopathy** — self-directed permitted up to 21 hrs (less the 4 mandatory).
5. **Medical (via RACGP/ACRRM)** — Educational Activities category explicitly includes "reading, lectures, podcasts" — up to 25 hrs/yr (12.5 EA + 12.5 flex) can be passive-style.
6. **Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander Health Practice** — up to 15 hrs self-directed.
7. **Medical Radiation Practice** — large general bucket within the 60/3-yr cycle.

### Boards with strict formal/interactive/peer minimums (LIMITED platform leverage — platform caps at allowed %)

The platform's value here is capped by mandatory interactive/peer/assessment requirements:

| Board | Hard interactive/peer minimum | Effective self-directed cap |
|---|---|---|
| **Psychology** | 10 hrs peer consultation (33%) | 20 hrs (67%) |
| **Paramedicine** | 8 hrs interactive (27%) | 22 hrs (73%) |
| **Optometry (therapeutic)** | 5 hrs interactive (17%) | 25 hrs (83%) |
| **Podiatry** | 5 hrs interactive (25%) | 15 hrs (75%) |
| **Occupational Therapy** | 5 hrs interactive (25%) | 15 hrs (75%) |
| **Chinese Medicine** | 5 hrs interactive + 4 hrs ethics (45%) | ~11 hrs (55%) |
| **Pharmacy** | Group 1 (passive) ≤ 50% of credits | Hard 50% ceiling |
| **Dental** | 80% must be clinical/scientific (not interactive per se, but content-restricted) | 20% non-clinical max |

For these Boards, the platform should expose the cap clearly: e.g., for a psychologist, surface "10 hrs of your 30 needs peer consultation — passive tracking covers your other 20 hrs."

### How auto-tracking should categorise activity (mapped to RACGP model — the most-cited CPD Home)

The platform should normalise every workflow event into one of three buckets and let the user adjust:

| Workflow event | Auto-category | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reading a clinical guideline / journal article | **Educational Activities (EA)** | Requires reflection note for full credit |
| Watching a webinar / podcast | **Educational Activities (EA)** | Pure passive — counts |
| Reviewing patient feedback / survey | **Reviewing Performance (RP)** | Needs reference to GP's own work |
| Receiving peer feedback / case discussion | **Reviewing Performance (RP)** | Document the peer and topic |
| Running an audit / chart review / mini-audit | **Measuring Outcomes (MO)** | Document the dataset and outcome |
| PDSA cycle, clinical guideline contribution | **Measuring Outcomes (MO)** | Outcome data required |
| Conference attendance | **Educational Activities (EA)** | Interactive sessions may also bank toward interactive minimums for non-medical Boards |
| Teaching / supervising | **Reviewing Performance (RP)** (ACRRM model) | Maps to RP under ACRRM; may need separate handling for RACGP |

Because RACGP and ACRRM are the two AMC-accredited Homes for medical practitioners, the EA / RP / MO triad is the canonical schema. Non-medical Boards either accept this implicitly (NMBA, Physio, Osteo, Chiro) or layer an interactive minimum on top (OT, Podiatry, Paramedicine, Chinese Medicine, ATSIHP, Optometry).

### Boards with the strictest recording requirements (platform audit-export feature most valuable)

These Boards explicitly mandate portfolios with goals + reflection + outcome assessment. The platform's audit-export feature solves a real pain point:

1. **Paramedicine** — explicit "portfolio MUST document learning goals, planned activities AND reflection on practice improvement". Annual random audit stated by Board.
2. **Optometry** — mandatory CPD portfolio + Learning Plan; 5-yr retention.
3. **Occupational Therapy** — portfolio documenting learning goals, planned activities, reflection on practice improvement.
4. **Psychology** — peer consultation must be evidenced with signed records.
5. **Medical (via CPD Home)** — every doctor must hold a Professional Development Plan (PDP). PDP-aligned activity tagging is a clear platform feature.
6. **Chinese Medicine** — explicit 5-yr retention; portfolio + any other Board-requested evidence.

### Headline numbers to never overstate

- "60–80% earned passively" is **accurate for** NMBA, Physiotherapy, Chiropractic, Osteopathy, and ATSIHP. It is **overstated for** Psychology (67% cap), Pharmacy (50% cap), Chinese Medicine (~55% cap), Dental (technically uncapped on activity type but 80% must be clinical/scientific content).
- "100% passive" is **only true for** NMBA and Physiotherapy. Every other Board has at least an interactive minimum, a peer minimum, an assessment minimum, or a content-type minimum.
- For RACGP/ACRRM specifically, the safe claim is: **"Up to 25 of your 50 hours can be earned through reading and self-directed learning under Educational Activities and the flex bucket."** RP and MO genuinely require feedback-on-self and data-on-self respectively.
