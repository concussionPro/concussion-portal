## 2. Full store description

Two versions below. Both stay under 4,000 characters. Content is identical in
substance; the Apple version drops the tag-style closing line Google allows.

### 2a. Apple App Store — Description

```
SST Trainer delivers clinician-directed, sub-symptom-threshold aerobic exercise
for people recovering from concussion. Your clinician measures your heart-rate
threshold in the clinic, then SST Trainer holds each home session inside the band
they prescribe — on a heart-rate wearable you already own.

This app is used under a clinician's direction. You start by entering the clinic
code your clinician gives you; there is no self-guided or self-diagnosis mode.

HOW IT WORKS
1. In clinic, your clinician runs a guided graded test to find your heart-rate
   threshold — the point where symptoms begin to intensify.
2. From that measured threshold, the app sets a training band (about 80–90% of
   the threshold) with a hard ceiling at the threshold itself.
3. At home, each session runs warm-up, a steady main set held inside the band,
   then cool-down — with a live heart-rate gauge and clear stop-rules if
   symptoms rise.
4. Your clinician reviews your sessions and re-tests over time to progress the
   band as you recover.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A clinic code from a clinician who has enrolled you.
- A compatible heart-rate source in Bluetooth "broadcast mode" — most Garmin,
  Polar, WHOOP, Coros, Suunto and Wahoo watches broadcast a standard Bluetooth
  Low Energy Heart Rate signal, as do Bluetooth chest straps and armbands.
- IMPORTANT: Apple Watch and Fitbit do NOT broadcast a standard Bluetooth Heart
  Rate signal, so they cannot stream live to this app. If your wearable can't
  broadcast, the app has a manual heart-rate entry mode so you can still train
  to your prescribed band by checking your watch and entering the reading.
- This app connects only to the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate broadcast. It does
  not connect to Garmin Connect, Fitbit, the WHOOP app, Strava or Apple Health,
  and it does not read your health records.

FOR CLINICIANS
SST Trainer is the between-visit supervision layer for the sub-symptom-threshold
protocol you already use. You measure the threshold with a guided graded test;
the app holds every home session in your prescribed band, capped at the measured
threshold, and surfaces symptom flares and verified-session progression so
follow-ups run on real data rather than recall. You direct testing, prescription
and re-test — the app does not diagnose, treat, or make return-to-play decisions.
Enrol patients with your clinic code.

THE EVIDENCE (provenance)
The method is the Buffalo Concussion Treadmill Test approach to finding a measured
heart-rate threshold (Leddy, Willer and colleagues), delivering the sub-symptom-
threshold aerobic exercise that the 6th International Consensus on Concussion in
Sport (Amsterdam 2023) endorses as first-line care. SST Trainer applies that
established protocol; it does not itself claim to speed or guarantee recovery.

IMPORTANT
SST Trainer is a coaching and tracking tool for use under a clinician's direction.
It is not a medical device and does not diagnose concussion, treat concussion, or
provide return-to-play clearance. Always follow your clinician's advice, and stop
and contact your clinician if symptoms worsen.
```

## 5. Privacy disclosures (CORRECTED to reflect reality)

Ground truth this reflects: in **clinic mode** the app collects the **patient's
name** and **live heart-rate data**, both **linked to the patient's identity via
the clinic code**; live HR is **streamed to a key-value store** during sessions
and reviewed by the clinician. Manual-entry tier still produces heart-rate values
tied to the same enrolment. No advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs.

### 5a. Apple Privacy "Nutrition Label"

**Data Used to Track You** *(tracking = sharing with data brokers / cross-app ad tracking)*
- **NONE.** The app does not track users across apps or websites and shares no
  data with data brokers or ad networks.

**Data Linked to You** *(collected and tied to identity — here, via name + clinic code)*
- **Health & Fitness → Health:** heart-rate readings (live-streamed and/or
  manually entered) and session records. *Purpose: App Functionality.*
- **Contact Info → Name:** patient name entered at clinic enrolment.
  *Purpose: App Functionality.*
- **Identifiers → User ID:** clinic code / patient enrolment identifier that links
  a patient to their clinician. *Purpose: App Functionality.*
- **User Content → Other User Content:** symptom ratings / stop-rule responses
  entered during sessions. *Purpose: App Functionality.*
- **[LAWYER]** If any diagnostic-adjacent free-text note is stored, it must be
  disclosed here too — confirm what the session/flare records actually capture.

**Data Not Linked to You**
- **Diagnostics / Usage Data:** only if crash or performance telemetry is enabled
  in the shipped build. **[VERIFY]** — confirm whether the release build carries
  any crash-reporting or analytics SDK; if yes, disclose here; if it is tied to
  the user, move it to *Data Linked to You*. Default assumption for v1: none.

Apple flags to set truthfully in App Store Connect:
- "Do you or your third-party partners use data for tracking?" → **No.**
- Health data present → confirm the app does **not** use Apple HealthKit (it does
  not) and that health data is collected **directly via Bluetooth / manual entry**,
  not read from Apple Health.

### 5b. Google Play Data Safety

**Data collected / shared**
- **Health and fitness → Health info:** heart-rate readings + session records.
  Collected: **Yes.** Shared: **No** (reviewed by the enrolling clinician within
  the service; this is not third-party "sharing" in Google's sense — **[LAWYER]**
  confirm the clinician-review relationship is documented as data processing, not
  "sharing", in the privacy policy).
- **Personal info → Name:** collected Yes, shared No.
- **App activity / Other user-generated content:** symptom ratings collected Yes,
  shared No.
- **Device or other IDs → clinic/enrolment identifier:** collected Yes, shared No.

**Security & handling answers**
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** → **Yes** (must be true — the live-HR stream
  to the key-value store and all API calls must be TLS. **[VERIFY]** end-to-end
  before ticking this box.)
- **Can users request data deletion?** → **Yes** — provide the mechanism (patient
  asks the clinic; clinic deletes) and a deletion-request URL/email. **[LAWYER]**
  confirm the deletion path exists and is documented.
- **Is this data required or optional?** → Name + clinic code **required** to use
  clinic mode; heart-rate data **required** for training function.
- **Committed to Play Families policy?** → **[LAWYER]** — adolescents are a real
  user group (SRC cohorts skew young). Confirm age-gating / parental-consent posture
  and whether the app targets children; this changes the Data Safety + Families
  answers materially.

### 5c. Privacy disclosures needing a lawyer's eyes **[LAWYER]**
1. **Health data of minors.** Concussion patients are frequently under 18. Consent,
   parental authority, and Play Families / App Store Kids-category posture all turn
   on this. Highest-priority item.
2. **Clinician review as "processing" vs "sharing."** The name + HR are visible to
   the enrolling clinic. The privacy policy must frame the clinic as a data
   controller/processor relationship so it isn't a third-party "sale/share."
3. **Live-HR key-value store retention.** How long streamed HR persists in the KV
   store, where it is hosted (data residency — AU health data), and deletion SLA.
4. **Australian Privacy Principles + health-record obligations.** Health info is
   "sensitive information" under the Privacy Act 1988; APP 3/6 consent and APP 11
   security apply. Needs a compliant privacy policy URL linked in both stores.
5. **In-transit + at-rest encryption claim.** Do not tick "encrypted" until verified
   in the shipped build.
