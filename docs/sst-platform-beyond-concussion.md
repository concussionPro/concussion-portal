# SST Platform — Beyond Concussion (Expansion Thesis)

**Status:** thesis / not started. Concussion is the beachhead; this is the market that justifies the tool.
**Date:** 2026-07-22

## The reframe

SST Trainer is not fundamentally a concussion tool. It is a **measured-HR-threshold graded-exercise-reconditioning + wearable-monitoring engine**. Concussion is one application of a general pattern: *exercise intolerance from autonomic/cardiovascular dysregulation, treated by graded sub-threshold aerobic reconditioning, progressed on measured HR.* The 25-supplier ACC channel is a beachhead (~NZ$170k/yr TAM at full penetration); the engine's real market is autonomic / exercise-intolerance rehab.

## Vertical 1: POTS / dysautonomia (the first expansion)

**Why it's the right first vertical:** the standard-of-care POTS exercise treatment is *already* a measured-HR-threshold, recumbent→upright, ~3-month graded reconditioning program — structurally ~1:1 with the SST engine. And it's badly delivered (a PDF packet + a chest strap in a physician's office), with a documented ~41% dropout that a monitored, adherence-supporting app directly attacks.

**Market (advocacy-org estimates — mark as estimates, not epidemiology):** POTS ~1–3M Americans pre-COVID, "doubled" post-pandemic (Dysautonomia International); ~80% female, age 15–50; ~25% too disabled to work. Managed across cardiology (EP), autonomic/neurology, Long-COVID and physio clinics; specialist autonomic clinics scarce → long diagnostic delays. Treatment side thin.

**Evidence / protocol layer (net-new — this is NOT the concussion/Leddy base):**
- Fu, Levine et al. 2011, *Hypertension* — exercise training **beat propranolol** in POTS. DOI 10.1161/HYPERTENSIONAHA.111.172262
- George, Levine et al. 2016, *Heart Rhythm* — international POTS registry, community delivery: of completers **~71% no longer met POTS criteria**; standing-HR delta 46→23 bpm; **59% completion (~41% dropout).** DOI 10.1016/j.hrthm.2015.12.012
- **CHOP Modified Dallas POTS Exercise Program** — 8 monthly calendars; Months 1–3 recumbent/seated (recumbent bike, rower, swim), Month 4 upright bike, Month 5 upright, Months 6–8 incline/HIIT; **individually prescribed HR zones** (from a max exercise test, not an age formula); mandatory next-day recovery below zone; heat caution.

**What carries over from the concussion build:** the whole engine — graded test → measured HR threshold → sub-threshold prescription → wearable-monitored home sessions → serial re-test trajectory; the clinic-code/clinician-directed access model; report/adherence tooling; the "measured > estimated HR threshold" wedge.

**What's net-new:**
1. **Position-progression axis** — recumbent→upright over months (concussion has no position axis).
2. **Standing-HR / orthostatic delta** as the trajectory outcome (vs symptom-threshold in concussion). Absorb a **NASA 10-min lean / stand test** as the low-tech orthostatic assessment (no tilt table).
3. **Beta-blocker RPE fallback** — many POTS patients are on rate-control drugs that blunt HR; the tool needs an RPE-driven path when HR is unreliable.
4. **The PEM screen + hard exclusion (below).**

## THE HARD SAFETY GATE — ME/CFS / post-exertional malaise (build-blocking)

**Non-negotiable.** NICE NG206 (2021) says do **NOT** offer graded exercise therapy to people with ME/CFS — incremental exercise triggers **post-exertional malaise (PEM)** and can cause lasting deterioration; the sanctioned approach is pacing/energy-management, never automatic increase.

- **POTS ≠ ME/CFS.** POTS is orthostatic intolerance / deconditioning — reconditioning *improves* it. ME/CFS is defined by PEM — reconditioning *harms* it.
- **The populations overlap heavily** (fatigue, exercise intolerance, female skew, Long-COVID origin; many carry both labels). A graded tool that can't tell them apart is a genuine harm risk.
- **The gate:** screen for **PEM at intake and continuously** (validated instrument — DSQ-PEM subscale); **hard-exclude** anyone meeting ME/CFS criteria or reporting PEM; **halt-and-reassess** if graded loading provokes delayed post-exertional crashes (the recovery-day/trajectory data can surface this). Market as **"for POTS / orthostatic intolerance without PEM"** — never "for chronic fatigue." Same exclusion discipline as the NeuroVision JME playbook.

## Competitive landscape — the lane is open

- **Visible (makevisible.com)** — the funded incumbent, and philosophically **OPPOSITE.** Wearable (Polar-made Band) + app for energy-limiting chronic illness (Long COVID, ME/CFS, POTS, fibro, EDS); **100k+ members**; "PacePoints" energy-budgeting that keeps users **UNDER** threshold to **avoid** PEM. Mount Sinai / Oxford researchers, wide press. Visible serves the PEM population a graded tool must *exclude* — so it **validates the wearable-for-chronic-illness market while leaving the reconditioning lane open.** Clean line: **Visible keeps you under threshold; SST rebuilds you toward it.** (Funding amount unverified — don't cite a figure.)
- **Dedicated POTS reconditioning apps:** effectively none at scale — Dallas/CHOP is still a PDF + chest strap. **This is the open lane.**
- **Cardiac-rehab remote-monitoring platforms** (Moving Analytics/Movn, Recora, Pattern Health): structurally the closest analog (HR-guided home reconditioning) but built for post-MI/HF reimbursement, not autonomic — a possible partnership/acquisition frame, not a competitor for this population.
- Stand-test / symptom-diary apps (Standing Up to POTS et al.) are assessment/logging primitives to absorb, not treatment competitors.

## Regulatory read (direction, not legal advice)

Risk rises modestly vs concussion-monitoring (cardiovascular condition; the intervention drives HR up → syncope/arrhythmia/wrongly-included-PEM events more foreseeable). **Framing is the lever:** a clinician-directed monitoring + adherence + data-capture tool ("supports the clinician's prescription; displays HR against clinician-set zones; logs sessions") is lower-risk (wellness/CDS / low-class SaMD) than one that diagnoses POTS or auto-titrates dose. Keep prescription authority with the clinician (as the clinic-code model already does). The PEM exclusion is also a **regulatory asset** — a documented, guideline-aligned risk control. Confirm via the same SaMD counsel path as SST.

## Other verticals (same engine, later)

Cardiac rehab (mature, reimbursed, crowded — partnership not build), pulmonary rehab, general deconditioning, the autonomic slice of Long COVID (careful — overlaps PEM). POTS is the cleanest first extension.

## Sequencing (don't split focus)

1. **Prove the engine in concussion** — ACC reference + international CRM = revenue + credibility on a defined evidence base + clean regulatory footing.
2. **Then extend to POTS** — reuse the engine wholesale; add the POTS protocol layer, the beta-blocker RPE path, the orthostatic assessment, and the PEM gate. Own "measured-threshold graded reconditioning for POTS," the niche Visible left open.
3. Bank cardiac/pulmonary as later platform plays or partnerships.

**Single biggest risk:** including an ME/CFS/PEM patient and harming them — clinical, reputational, and regulatory at once. The PEM exclusion is the thing that makes the product defensible, not polish.

## Real volume — sourced (2026-07-22)

**HARD (peer-reviewed):**
- **Shaw et al. 2019, *J Intern Med* (PMID 30861229)** — n=4,835; **94% female**; onset mode **age 14**; **median diagnostic delay 24 months (IQR 6–72).** Use this for demographics + the delay story (the older "sees 7 doctors / 4+ years" line is a softer survey figure — Shaw's verified median is 2 yrs).
- **Kwan et al. 2022, *Nature Cardiovascular Research* (PMID 37303827), n=284,592** — POTS odds **~5× higher after SARS-CoV-2 infection.** The defensible "Long COVID expanded the market" anchor.

**SOFT (advocacy — label as estimate):** 1–3M US pre-COVID, "doubled" post-COVID, ~80% female, onset 15–50, ~25% can't work (Dysautonomia International). No clean population-prevalence paper exists.

Do not quote AU numbers — it's genuine white-space, not a quantified market.

## Monetization — corrected

**Clinician-only, and the education is bundled — same three-part model as concussion.** CEA is an educator + tool supplier, not a tool vendor. The POTS offer to a clinic/clinician mirrors CCM+SST+protocol:
- **Education (net-new to build):** a POTS/autonomic clinician course — the Dallas/CHOP method, orthostatic assessment, the beta-blocker RPE path, and the PEM exclusion. This is the competency/credential piece (one-off per clinician, CPD-bearing) and the wedge into the hubs.
- **Tool:** SST in POTS mode — per-clinician recurring subscription (delivers + monitors the reconditioning). The recurring line.
- **Protocol:** a POTS standard, published/citable — free, underwrites both.

Never direct-to-patient. Sell on **clinical outcomes + protocol fidelity + clinician time-leverage**, NOT reimbursement. **RTM is a stretch/false** (no autonomic code; MSK-reframe carries audit risk; US-only; PT-billability contested) — hedged US-only upside at most, never the core ROI. The hubs below are the **distribution for the bundled education+tool** — e.g. Dysautonomia International already runs a PT/OT Rehab Intensive; supply/co-brand the education content into it with the tool as the delivery instrument. Pharma trial-instrument = a real high-margin niche later.

## Market-structure reality check — there is NO "ACC for POTS" (validate distribution BEFORE building)

Concussion works because ACC is a central authority that mandates a service, holds the clinician network through contracted suppliers, and leaves the method undefined — a concentrated buyer to sell a standard into. **POTS has no equivalent.** Cross-specialty, mostly self-pay, no funder mandating a standardized service, no supplier→clinician chain. Bigger patient market, far more fragmented clinician-distribution market.

Candidates that could distribute a POTS+SST package to their clinicians, by how authority-like they are:
1. **Dysautonomia International — best available, opt-in not mandate.** Already runs an accredited **Rehab Intensive for PT/OT** = it distributes standardized POTS rehab education to clinicians today. Get the package endorsed/embedded there. But it educates independent opt-in clinicians — reach, not mandate.
2. **AACVPR — the most ACC-like structure, worth investigating.** Accredits cardiac/pulm rehab *programs* (real programs, staff clinicians, standards above them). If POTS reconditioning rode cardiac-rehab infrastructure, that's the ACC-shaped network. Catch: AACVPR doesn't cover POTS today → a "get it into the rehab standard" play, slow/unproven.
3. **Large employers** (hospital autonomic/Long-COVID clinics, big physio groups) — B2B one at a time (the ACC-supplier model), no central authority above them.

**Decision rule:** DO NOT scope the POTS course build until a distribution partner commits. The cheap test that decides it: will DI (or a cardiac-rehab network via AACVPR) put a standardized reconditioning package in front of their clinicians? One bite → build. No channel commits → a course is content with no shelf.
