## Why Heidi Could Capture This Market Completely

### Three structural advantages no scribe competitor has

1. **Trust transfer.** Clinicians already trust Heidi with patient data. Trust transfers to education. Lyrebird, Halo can't replicate this.
2. **Daily engagement = discovery.** Surface CPD *in context* during workflow ("you just documented a complex case — here's a CPD module on the topic"). 10× conversion vs external sites.
3. **Automatic CPD tracking.** Industry-wide tracking solves the audit nightmare. No competitor offers it.

### Network effects
More users → more CPD providers want to list → better selection → more users.
**Defensible moat Lyrebird can't copy.**

## Honest Reality Check

### What makes this more likely to succeed
- Dr Condon is actively planning to expand Heidi (his "Care Partner" vision)
- Education / CPD fits as the "fourth act" he hasn't built yet
- Market is large ($480M) and fragmented (no dominant player)
- Proof-of-concept already built — lowers his risk
- Free pilot approach = no upfront commitment needed

### What could make this fail
- Heidi prioritises global expansion over new verticals (likely — they just raised $100M)
- Dr Condon doesn't see CPD as strategic (yet)
- Long sales cycle (3-6 months minimum even for free pilot)
- Internal resource constraints (his team too busy with core product)

### Base-case probability
| Outcome | Probability |
|---|---|
| "Interesting, but not now" (timing / priority issue) | **60%** |
| Free pilot approved (green light to test) | **25%** |
| Strategic partnership discussion (moves to licensing) | **10%** |
| Employment offer (rare from cold outreach) | **5%** |

### The realistic interpretation
The free pilot ask is structured precisely to make "yes" the cheapest answer Dr Condon can give. He doesn't need to allocate budget, doesn't need to approve a contract, doesn't need to involve legal. He needs to say "OK, give us 100 users to test." That's the question that has to be made easy.

If 60% comes back as "interesting, not now" — that's the expected outcome from a single cold email. The play is to nurture: monthly value drop (e.g. an AHPRA update digest, a new module preview), Heidi sees the maintenance pipeline in action, the relationship matures, the opportunity surfaces again at the next funding round or strategic-planning cycle.
