## TL;DR

**Primary contact: Paul Williamson, CRO.** Revenue opportunity → CRO territory. He owns partnerships, marketplace revenue, ecosystem deals. Joined Oct 2025, needs portfolio wins, fluent in platform economics (Plaid, Salesforce AppExchange).

**Secondary contact: Dr Ben Condon, Clinical Director.** Use only for clinical validation AFTER Paul has engaged on commercial terms, OR if a mutual contact provides a warm intro.

## Why NOT Dr Condon as primary

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Wrong domain** | Clinical governance, AHPRA/TGA relations, product strategy. Not commercial deals. |
| **Too strategic, not tactical** | He shapes the product roadmap. He doesn't close partnerships. |

Dr Condon's role in this pitch is **clinical validation** of CEA's content + AHPRA alignment — that conversation happens AFTER Paul Williamson signals commercial interest.

## Risk if this is wrong

If Paul Williamson isn't actually the right contact (e.g. he's narrowly focused on US enterprise sales and ANZ partnerships aren't on his desk), the pitch still has the same content — just wrong recipient. He'll forward internally. Worst case: 1-week delay vs going to Dr Condon directly.

**The downside of going Paul-first is bounded. The downside of going Condon-first is up to 6 months of strategic-roadmap discussion before commercial terms are even on the table.** Asymmetric risk → go Paul.
