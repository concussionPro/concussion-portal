## Pitch principles
1. **Lead with free.** The institution's decision is "accept a free athlete-welfare resource" — frictionless.
2. **Authority first.** "We train the clinicians" + OA endorsement + research + speaking. That's why an institution trusts a stranger with their athletes.
3. **Cost is the family's, at point of need.** Never frame a cost to the institution.
4. **Founder-paced, not blasted.** Elite schools/academies are relationship sells; the engine sources + sequences, Zac closes.
5. **Consent for the authority wall** — ask to list them as a partner; never display without sign-off.
