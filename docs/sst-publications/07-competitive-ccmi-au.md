# CCMI's Australian footprint and the openness of CEA's home lane

**A competitive scan**

Concussion Education Australia (CEA) — SST Trainer programme
Draft v1.0 · 2026-06-29

> **Purpose.** Establish, with evidence, whether Complete Concussion Management Inc. (CCMI) — and the CCMI+Wibbi digital concussion module — already occupies the Australian concussion-rehabilitation market CEA intends to enter, and whether the surrounding institutional space (SMA / RACGP / AIS) is filled or open. The honest conclusion drives positioning: SST Trainer is the **Australian-market, allied-health-education-anchored instantiation** of an established protocol, **not** a global platform play against CCMI+Wibbi.

---

## 1. Bottom line up front

**Verdict: the Australian lane is CONTESTED, not open and not closed.**

CCMI is *already present in Australia* as a clinical-certification network, with accredited/registered practices across Sydney and multiple capital cities, and its protocols are now embedded in a commercial digital exercise-prescription product (Wibbi) that is being marketed to all CCMI-affiliated clinics worldwide — including Australian ones — as of December 2025. The claim that *no one occupies the Australian concussion-rehab space* is false and must not be made anywhere in CEA's materials.

However, the lane is **contested rather than closed** because CCMI's Australian footprint is (a) concentrated in private sports-physiotherapy / chiropractic / sports-medicine clinics, predominantly in Sydney and the larger capitals; (b) positioned as a *clinic-certification + baseline-testing + protocol* network, not as an **Osteopathy-Australia-endorsed CPD authority** for the broader allied-health audience CEA serves; and (c) its digital tool (Wibbi) is a general home-exercise-program platform with a concussion *content module*, not a purpose-built, fail-closed, hardware-agnostic heart-rate-verification engine. The national institutional bodies (SMA, AIS/ASC, RACGP) occupy the *guidelines and recognition-tool* layer, not the *between-visit digital rehab-delivery* layer — that layer is where the genuine white space remains, and it is narrow.

CEA's defensible position is therefore **lane and moat, not first-ness**: the Australian allied-health-education channel (OA-endorsed CPD, osteopathy/EP/physio network) plus the verification-architecture moat — not a claim to have invented digital SSTAE delivery, which is dated and occupied.

---

## 2. Is CCMI in Australia? Yes — clearly and with depth.

CCMI describes itself as a global concussion network of 300+ clinic locations across multiple countries ([completeconcussions.com](https://completeconcussions.com/)). Its Australian presence is not theoretical — it is a live, marketed Partnership Program with named, certified practices:

- **Sydney Concussion Centre** — explicitly markets itself as a CCMI Accredited / Registered Practice, with practitioners holding "CCMI registered practitioner" status off the back of 50+ hours of post-university training and a competency exam ([sydneyconcussioncentre.com.au/ccmi](https://sydneyconcussioncentre.com.au/ccmi/), [/we-are-a-ccmi-registered-practice](https://sydneyconcussioncentre.com.au/we-are-a-ccmi-registered-practice/)).
- **St George SportsMed** (Sydney) — a CCMI Partnership Program clinic ([stgeorgesportsmed.com.au](https://www.stgeorgesportsmed.com.au/services/concussion-clinic/)).
- **Sports Lab** — a certified Complete Concussions provider offering the program across all its clinics ([sportslab.com.au](https://sportslab.com.au/services/physiotherapy/concussion-management)).
- **Peak Sports Physiotherapy** — CCMI concussion services ([peakphysio.com.au](https://www.peakphysio.com.au/ccmi-concussion-services/)).
- **Enhance Healthcare** — CCMI Partnership Program clinic ([enhance.com.au](https://www.enhance.com.au/concussion-assessment-and-managamen)).
- **SportsCare Canberra** — Complete Concussion Management ([sportscarephysio.com.au](https://sportscarephysio.com.au/complete-concussion-management)).
- **Barossa Physiotherapy** (SA) — CCMI concussion management ([barossa.physio](https://www.barossa.physio/ccmi-concussion-management)).
- **Melbourne Spine Clinic** — practitioners qualified in the Complete Concussions CCMI Baseline Testing Protocol ([melbournespineclinic.com.au](https://www.melbournespineclinic.com.au/concussion-treatment/)).
- **CXDX**, **CP Physio** (St Peters), **HH Physio and Sports Rehab** (Oatley) — further certified Sydney-area locations ([cxdx.au/ccmi](https://www.cxdx.au/ccmi)).

CCMI also runs an Australian-facing clinic locator with city pages (e.g. South Brisbane, Greater Sydney) ([clinics.completeconcussions.com](https://clinics.completeconcussions.com/in/south-brisbane-qld-au)), confirming an active, geographically distributed go-to-market, not a single token clinic.

**Read on profession mix:** the visible CCMI-AU footprint skews to **physiotherapy, chiropractic, and sports-medicine** clinics. Public materials did not surface CCMI as the *certifying education body of choice for osteopaths or accredited exercise physiologists*, nor as an Osteopathy-Australia-endorsed CPD provider. That distinction is the narrow gap CEA's channel exploits — but it is a channel/audience gap, not an absence of a competitor.

**Commercial model (for reference):** CCMI's clinic offering is a certification + software subscription (~US$1,500/yr per clinic plus a per-patient fee of roughly US$10), i.e. a clinic-certification business, not a direct-to-clinician CPD authority — again distinct from CEA's OA-endorsed education channel.

## 3. Is the CCMI+Wibbi digital module available in Australia? Yes — globally, as of Dec 2025.

On **4 December 2025**, CCMI and Wibbi announced a strategic partnership introducing a dedicated **concussion module inside the Wibbi exercise-prescription / home-exercise-program platform**, giving providers access to CCMI's evidence-based protocols, assessment tools, rehabilitation protocols and return-to-play guidance directly inside the Wibbi care-pathway builder with outcome tracking ([wibbi.com partnership announcement](https://wibbi.com/resource/complete-concussions-wibbi-partnership/)).

The commercial terms make clear this is being actively pushed to the existing certified base — *including Australian clinics*: all CCMI-affiliated clinics get 50% off Wibbi HEP for six months, and Wibbi-network providers get 20% off the CCMI training course. Because the CCMI-AU clinics listed in §2 are CCMI-affiliated, the concussion module is, in practice, **available and marketed to Australian providers now**.

**What this means for the "novelty of digital delivery" claim:** it is dead in its broad form. A commercial, clinician-facing, digitally-delivered concussion exercise-prescription product with outcome tracking exists and is reaching Australia. CEA cannot claim to be first to *digitally deliver* concussion exercise prescription.

**What it does NOT mean:** Wibbi's concussion leg is an **exercise-video + RPE-guided home-exercise-program** platform with care-pathway/outcome-tracking. The HRt and the sub-threshold prescription are **set in clinic (from the BCTT) on paper**; Wibbi delivers that program as videos and RPE, and — on the public partnership description — does **not** stream a live wearable heart-rate feed and gate the training band against the measured HRt. It is not described as a purpose-built **live-HR-monitored, heart-rate-threshold verification engine** with a fail-closed, never-fabricate-the-signal architecture and hardware-agnostic live BLE wearable sourcing (camera-PPG being a resting spot-check only, never the live exercise source). That specific engineering — live measured-HR zone delivery + the verification architecture, the moat the three papers and the patent memo are built around — is not what the CCMI+Wibbi module is. So the contest is on *digital exercise-prescription delivery in general* (lost) but not yet on *live HR-verified zone delivery and the verification architecture specifically* (open, and the basis of the moat).

## 4. The Australian institutional layer: guidelines, not delivery tools

The national bodies occupy the **standards/recognition** layer, not the **between-visit digital rehab-delivery** layer:

- **Sports Medicine Australia (SMA)** + **AIS / Australian Sports Commission (ASC)** co-published the *Concussion and Brain Health Position Statement 2024* and the *Australian Concussion Guidelines for Youth and Community Sport*, developed with ACSEP and the Australian Physiotherapy Association ([sma.org.au/resources/concussion](https://sma.org.au/resources/concussion/); [ausport.gov.au/concussion](https://www.ausport.gov.au/concussion)). Their conservative graded-return framework (14 days symptom-free before contact training; 21 days before competitive contact) is the AU policy backdrop SST Trainer must align to.
- Their *tools* are recognition/education aids — the **Concussion Recognition Tool 6 (CRT6)** for sideline recognition, posters, and education modules at [concussioninsport.gov.au](https://www.concussioninsport.gov.au) — **not** a between-visit, heart-rate-monitored aerobic-rehab delivery app. A holistic "Mind the Gap"-style technology model for clinician/patient education is referenced, but again sits at the education/framework layer.
- **RACGP / MJA:** new Australian & Aotearoa-NZ guidelines for mild TBI / persisting post-concussion symptoms were published in *MJA* (2025), reinforcing evidence-based assessment and management ([mja.com.au 2025;223(9)](https://www.mja.com.au/journal/2025/223/9/mild-traumatic-brain-injury-and-concussion-and-persisting-post-concussion)). Again: a *guideline*, not a delivery tool.

**Implication:** the AU institutional layer sets the rules and supplies recognition tools but leaves the *faithful between-visit aerobic-rehab delivery* layer to private providers. CCMI+Wibbi is moving into exactly that layer. The white space CEA can credibly claim is the *intersection* of (AU allied-health-education channel) × (verification-architecture engineering) — a narrow strip, not an empty market.

## 5. How open is the lane? — graded answer

| Layer | Who occupies it in AU | Openness to CEA |
|---|---|---|
| National guidelines / recognition tools (CRT6, position statements) | SMA, AIS/ASC, ACSEP, APA, RACGP/MJA | **Closed** (and not a target — CEA aligns to it) |
| Clinic certification + baseline testing + protocol network | **CCMI** (Sydney + capitals; physio/chiro/sports-med led) | **Contested** — CCMI established; osteo/EP-education channel under-served |
| Commercial digital concussion exercise-prescription delivery + outcome tracking (RPE/video-guided) | **CCMI+Wibbi** (global, reaching AU since Dec 2025; RPE/exercise-video, HRt set in clinic on paper); Rhea (estimated age-predicted-max HR, overseas) and other mHealth apps | **Contested→closing** — first-ness gone; differentiate on architecture |
| Live wearable-HR-verified sub-threshold zone delivery + fail-closed, hardware-agnostic HR-**verification** engine (never-fabricate-signal) as a standardised supervised workflow | No identified occupant | **Open (narrow)** — the moat; basis of the honest novelty claim |
| OA-endorsed AU allied-health concussion **CPD authority** channel | CEA (existing position) | **CEA's home advantage** — distribution, not technology |

**Net:** *contested.* CCMI is real, in Australia, and now digitally enabled. CEA's defensible ground is the **bottom two rows**: the verification-architecture moat carried over CEA's existing OA-endorsed allied-health distribution channel — the lane CCMI's North-American, physio-clinic-certification model does not directly occupy.

---

## 6. Positioning consequences (what the other documents must reflect)

1. **Delete every "first / only / no existing tool" claim** about digital SSTAE/concussion-exercise delivery across papers 01–03 and the JOSS-style statements. Dated public prior art (Rhea app, mHealth monitoring studies, the Buffalo wearable-monitored trials) and a live commercial competitor (CCMI+Wibbi) defeat it.
2. **Reframe novelty to the narrow, honest form** — the *combination* of a fail-closed HR-verification engine (refuses to fabricate a reading) + hardware-agnostic, clinician-supervised, **BCTT-derived-HRt** (not age-predicted-max) delivery, as a standardised five-stage workflow. Frame the **framework paper (03)** as the safest novelty (standardisation, not first-ness).
3. **Position CEA as the endorsed Australian concussion-rehab authority**, with SST Trainer as the AU-market instantiation of an established protocol — explicitly *not* a CCMI competitor for the global platform crown (CCMI+Wibbi own that), but the tool that brings verified, supervised delivery to CEA's own OA-endorsed osteo/EP/physio education network.
4. **Patent caution (see `08-patent-prior-art-memo.md`):** broad "digital/wearable HR-threshold concussion prescription" claims are exposed by the same prior art and the CCMI+Wibbi product. Narrow to the verification architecture before the provisional converts.
