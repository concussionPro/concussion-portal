## The bodies (ordered as in the source)

1. **ACSM — Education Provider (USA).** Right to award ACSM CECs for a pre-approved course. Apply: `acsm.formstack.com/forms/acsm_education_provider_application_2024`. ≤4wk, 1yr per-course term. Contact `education@acsm.org`. Fee **[ENQUIRE]** est. USD $300–600 (in the form). Rubric auto-rejects programs that directly compete with ACSM → state non-competition early (no ACSM cert covers concussion). Watch: rubric PDF bot-blocked, open in browser. Blocks B1–B7. Full detail: [acsm-provider-application.md](acsm-provider-application.md).

2. **CIMSPA — Training Provider Partner + product endorsement (UK).** Directory listing + partner logo. Fees **[CONFIRMED]**: £140+VAT admin (bank transfer BEFORE review) · £280+VAT annual licence · £280+VAT per endorsed product. **Yr1 single product £700+VAT; subsequent ~£560+VAT** [VERIFY re-endorsement rate]. Endorsement runs in SUBMISSION WINDOWS not rolling — check the window before paying the admin fee. Demand driver: CIMSPA members need 10 CPD pts/yr, min 5 from CIMSPA-endorsed providers; 300+ employer partners buy endorsed-only. Precedent: Cancer United (single-condition specialist). Blocks B1–B8.

3. **CASES (ex-BASES) — CPD Endorsement (UK).** CASES credits logo + monthly e-newsletter + quarterly magazine + ~53k social promo. Min 2wk, annual. Contact `education@cases.org.uk`. Fee **[ENQUIRE]** est. £300–600, rejected apps refunded minus £50. Max 10 CPD credits/event; must suit a UK setting (B8) + be stand-alone. NOTE: the £1,800+VAT figure on their site is DEGREE endorsement (CUES), NOT CPD — don't budget from it. Blocks B1–B8.

4. **CEP-UK / AHCS — targeted outreach, no application (UK).** Not a scheme — a funded buyer list: 164 registered via portfolio/equivalence; pathway CLOSES 31 Dec 2026; **up to £650/individual** to meet CPD for AHCS CEP registration. Fee £0. **Highest conversion-per-effort in the doc** — small, identified, budget-holding, time-pressured, published competency framework (B9). Outreach only.

5. **CSEP — Professional Development Credits (Canada).** Route 1 (free): members self-report via PDC Tracker — works today, $0. Route 2 (formal): accreditation request to promote to CSEP Certified members + brand files, Fee [ENQUIRE] est. CAD $0–500. ~5,000 CPT/CEP. Contact `info@csep.ca`. **CSEP already grants equivalency to ESSA AEP/AES** → fire Route 2 the day ESSA lands (ESSA carries disproportionate weight here).

6. **BASA / HPCSA — Biokinetics (South Africa).** CPD accreditation + provider network via BASA; CEUs statutory under HPCSA. ~1,831 HPCSA biokineticists / ~1,600 BASA. Contact `basa@biokineticssa.org.za` (Phumzile Tjiane). Fee [ENQUIRE] est. R2,000–5,000 (~USD $110–280). **Exceptional scope fit** — biokineticist = specialised exercise therapist doing assessment + exercise prescription in clinical+performance context; HR-threshold rehab sits dead centre. Statutory CPD, English, no concussion competition. Bonus: BASA Life Through Movement regional conferences (9 provinces, 750+ delegates) = speaking-slot channel. Adjust CRM price for ZAR PPP.

7. **SESNZ / CEPNZ (New Zealand).** No formal provider program — listing on SESNZ CPD Opportunities page by request. Low hundreds; 20 CPD pts/yr; scheme modelled on ESSA's. Contact `natmanager@sesnz.org.nz` (CC CEPNZ). Fee [ENQUIRE] likely free. Reinforces the NZ/ACC work already in train.

8. **ESSA (Australia) — in flight.** PD submission final stages. On landing: update all copy, trigger CSEP Route 2, add credential to every other application here.

9. **COKO / Ontario kinesiologists — parked.** ~2,960 regulated RKins but the College runs QA, NOT a provider-endorsement scheme — no application to make. [VERIFY] whether the Ontario Kinesiology Association runs a listing scheme. Park until Canada is warm via CSEP.
