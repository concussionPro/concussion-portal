# Passive CPD — The Un-Copyable Insight

**Status:** Strategic insight, drives all pitch artefacts
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-22

---

## The insight

Clinicians do 5-10 literature searches per week — guideline lookups, drug interaction checks, case research, evidence reviews. Each search is 20-60 minutes. Annual total: **100-400 hours of CPD-qualifying research activity.**

They only need 50 hours/year for AHPRA.

**They never log it.** Manual CPD tracking has too much friction — by the time they finish researching the case, they've moved to the next patient. End of year: panic-complete formal courses to fill the gap.

## What Heidi can do that no scribe competitor can

Literature search already happens inside Heidi (via Evidence). Time tracking is native to the platform. With one feature addition:

```
Clinician completes a PubMed search → Heidi shows a discreet prompt:
"You spent 45 min researching type 2 diabetes outcomes. Log as CPD?"
[Yes — auto-categorise]  [Not now]  [Don't ask again]
```

One tap → auto-categorised → audit-ready entry on the CPD dashboard.

**Result: 30-40 hours of CPD earned passively. Clinician only needs 10-20 hours of formal courses to hit AHPRA's 50-hour requirement.**

## Why no competitor can copy this

| Competitor | Why they can't |
|---|---|
| Medcast | Tracks courses *on Medcast*. Can't see PubMed searches, guideline reviews, case research done elsewhere. To copy, they'd need clinicians to do all research inside Medcast — behavioural shift = impossible. |
| Lyrebird Health | Scribe-only. No literature search surface. No native time-tracking of research activity. |
| RACGP / ACRRM | Track only their member-published courses. Don't see workflow activity. |
| Halo Health | Scribe + small clinical-tools surface. No literature/evidence integration at scale. |
| Stand-alone CPD trackers | Manual logging only. Friction kills usage. |

Heidi's advantage compounds:
- **Research already happens in Heidi** (Evidence product launched Feb 2026)
- **Time tracking is native** (platform observes session length)
- **Heidi sees the 100-400 hours/year Medcast can't see**
