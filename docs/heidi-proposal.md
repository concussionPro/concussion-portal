## What we discussed

Heidi already owns the daily workflow for ~15,000 Australian clinicians. Those clinicians collectively spend 100-400 hours/year doing CPD-qualifying activity inside the platform (literature searches, guideline reviews, case research) that never gets logged. End of year, they panic-buy formal courses to fill the audit gap.

The fix is a two-layer product:
1. **Passive CPD capture** — discreet prompts after research sessions, one-tap log, auto-categorisation. Captures 60-80% of the AHPRA 50-hour annual requirement.
2. **Curated CPD marketplace** — vetted providers fill the residual 10-20-hour gap. Heidi takes 25% commission.

Revenue trajectory:
- Year 1 (current 15k AU users): **$6M GMV → $1.5M ARR to Heidi**
- Year 2 (50k target): **$20M GMV → $5M ARR**
- Year 3 (global expansion): **$60M GMV → $15M ARR**

Strategic value beyond revenue:
- **Retention moat.** Clinicians with 30+ logged CPD hours have 12-month switching cost. Lose Heidi, lose the audit record.
- **Enterprise lever.** "Your staff earn CPD by doing their job" closes the procurement conversation.
- **Competitive defence.** Abridge, Nabla, DeepScribe don't have CPD strategy. First-mover captures the category.

This proposal outlines three structures we could use to realise that opportunity, ordered from lowest to highest commitment.

**Structure**
- Heidi promotes CEA's AHPRA-aligned compliance course to AU users
- 70/30 revenue share (70% to CEA, 30% to Heidi)
- CEA retains all IP, content, and platform infrastructure
- Heidi provides distribution

**Year-1 revenue (rough)**
- 1% of 15k AU users convert at A$297 = 150 sales × A$297 × 30% = **~A$13k to Heidi**
- Continues at that scale unless conversion lifts significantly

**Cost to Heidi**: zero capex, marginal opex
**Cost to CEA**: content + cert maintenance (sunk cost)
**Time to close**: 4-6 weeks

**Verdict**: safest opening, but **leaves $20M on the table**. Heidi takes the productivity-tool fee; doesn't capture the marketplace position.

## My recommendation

**Option C.** Reasons:

1. **Speed.** Dedicated owner with Heidi's engineering resources ships in 6 months what would take CEA 18 months alone.
2. **Scale.** Heidi's global distribution (UK, NHS, US expansion already in motion) means the CPD vertical scales globally, not just AU. The platform is country-agnostic; the content is country-specific (we vet local providers per market).
3. **Moat.** 12-18 month lead over Abridge / Nabla / DeepScribe. First-mover captures the category; the marketplace network effects compound.
4. **Equity alignment.** If the CPD vertical adds A$15M ARR at scale, that's A$150M+ in valuation impact (10× ARR multiple for verticals like this). Even 0.1% participation in that creates A$150k+ in incremental founder value. 0.3% creates A$450k+.
5. **Risk-asymmetric.** If the CPD bet doesn't work out, Heidi has paid A$165k for a year of one senior hire — recoverable. If it does work out, Heidi captures a $5-15M ARR vertical no scribe competitor has.

Option A is too small. Option B has no upside-alignment. **Option C is the only structure where both sides win big.**
