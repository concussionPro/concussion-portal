## 1. What we're applying for
ACSM **Education Provider (Approved Provider)** — lets a third party award ACSM CECs for a pre-approved course.
- Portal: `acsm.formstack.com/forms/acsm_education_provider_application_2024`
- Reviewer: CCRB Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Sub-committee · Turnaround ≤4 weeks · Term 1 yr per-course, annually renewable
- On approval: approval letter, official statement wording, provider logo, certificate, listing on ACSM Approved Providers page
- Contact: `education@acsm.org` · Fee: not published, revealed in the Formstack form **[VERIFY on open]**
- **Auto-reject trap:** rubric auto-rejects programs that DIRECTLY COMPETE with ACSM. Concussion is covered by NO ACSM certification/specialty cert → we are complementary. Make this unmissable in the copy.

## 2. The gap (strategic core — do not bury)
- **2.1 Paradigm inverted:** Amsterdam 2022 (Patricios et al., BJSM 57(11):695–711) replaced rest with early sub-symptom-threshold aerobic exercise; ~4.6-day mean recovery improvement; ≤2-point symptom bump <1h is not harmful; cervicovestibular rehab combined with aerobic.
- **2.2 Gated on exercise testing:** the consensus first-line treatment is an **HR-threshold exercise prescription derived from a graded exercise test** — the definitional core competency of an exercise physiologist. The profession best equipped to deliver it is the one least trained in the condition.
- **2.3 ACSM published the call, didn't answer it:** ACSM Hot Topic *Exercise & Rest in Concussion Recovery* (23 Apr 2025, Hildenbrand & Herring FACSM) names FITT-based concussion exercise prescription as essential future work — yet ACSM's CEC catalogue has NO concussion course and the ~58-provider Approved Provider list has ZERO concussion providers.
- **2.4 One-line gap:** *Exercise professionals are now the consensus-designated deliverers of first-line concussion treatment, and there is no ACSM-CEC training that teaches them to do it.*
- **2.5 Competitors don't fill it:** CCMI (~$750, chiro closed ecosystem, not ACSM, assessment tools not HR-threshold rehab); Shift (~CAD $448–904, self-report CE, no bundled tooling); BIAA/ACBIS (awareness-level, no tools); Concussion&CTE Foundation (free, physician-targeted). Nobody sells an ACSM-CEC concussion course; nobody bundles sub-threshold training delivery. Slot empty + uncontested.

## 8b. Six plays, ordered by leverage-per-effort (2026-07-20)
1. **Quote ACSM to ACSM in the application.** The CPE sub-committee scores mission alignment — so cite ACSM's OWN published position (Herring & Hildenbrand naming FITT-based concussion exercise prescription as essential work + telling clinicians to stay current), then state CRM IS that training. A reviewer can't easily rate "doesn't serve our audience" against their own Honor Award recipient's article. One paragraph, highest return.
2. **Reframe the sale from topic → scope.** Not "a concussion course." Sell: the consensus now designates exercise testing as the gate to first-line concussion treatment — you already own that skill, this makes it billable in a new patient population. EPs/CEPs are chronically under-utilised + know it; a service-line expansion sells harder than a knowledge upgrade. Rewrite the landing headline + first 100 words of the ACSM listing copy around this.
3. **Answer the call in print.** ACSM named FITT-for-concussion the open frontier; SST is FITT operationalised (freq, intensity via HR threshold, time, type, progressed against symptom response). Write it → place in ACSM's Health & Fitness Journal or the Translational Journal of ACSM. Gives a citable asset inside ACSM's ecosystem + inbound discovery + positioning before the next consensus cycle. Slower, higher ceiling.
4. **CEPA as a distribution channel (post-approval).** The Clinical Exercise Physiology Association is ACSM-affiliated + runs its own ACSM-approved CEC e-learning for exactly the target credential. Once approved, co-listing or a webinar into their membership reaches CEPs pre-qualified for the scope argument. Queue post-approval (the volume-model upside trigger).
5. **The authors — low probability, low cost.** Hildenbrand is the realistic approach (NATA board, program director, on record about the gap): a short, specific invitation to REACT to how CRM operationalises what she wrote — not a pitch. Herring is a long shot; cost of one email is nil. Neither load-bearing; treat any response as upside.
6. **The same argument travels.** The Amsterdam exercise-testing gate is a FUNDING argument as much as a training one — a treatment that requires a testable/prescribable/progressable protocol. Reuse the paragraph across NZ/ACC and the CSEP pitch (once ESSA lands). Write it once, properly.

**Guardrail:** citing ACSM's article = fair use of a public position. Implying ACSM ENDORSES CRM is not — after approval you're limited to the specific statement wording they issue with the logo. Keep those separate in every asset; conflating them costs the listing at renewal. (Same discipline as ESSA_ACCREDITED — never claim an accreditation not yet held.)

**Fastest sequence:** rewrite the positioning paragraph (play 2) → drop into the application (play 1) → submit → draft the article during the review window (play 3).
