# AI in Clinical Practice — Pricing Scope

**Author:** Zac Lewis · CEA
**Date:** 22 May 2026
**Status:** Recommendation — subject to your sign-off

Pricing is set against compliance value, not productivity value. Buyers (clinicians, clinic owners, indemnity insurers, AI software partners) tolerate higher prices when the alternative is regulatory exposure.

---

## Anchors that justify the price

| Cost of non-compliance | Estimated AUD |
|---|---|
| AHPRA complaint investigation (clinician time + legal + lost income) | $2–15k |
| Single Notifiable Data Breach (NDB) remediation + reputational impact | $50–300k |
| Indemnity premium repricing after an AI-related claim | $1–5k/yr ongoing |

Course + Hub at $297 + $97/yr is rounding error against a single avoided incident.

## Pricing posture — what NOT to do

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Listing the course at sub-$100 | Anchors it as productivity SaaS, not compliance training. Buyers don't take compliance seriously below $150. |
| Free trial with no time limit | Erodes the certificate value. Allow Module 1 preview only (10 min). |
| Discounting heavily in launch | Compliance buyers don't shop on discount. They shop on perceived authority. |
| Per-month subscription for the course alone | Breaks the "I bought it, I'm certified" narrative. Hub can be subscription; course itself is one-time. |
| Charging Heidi a per-user fee that scales linearly with their user base | They'll churn. Charge a base + bounty so growth aligns. |
