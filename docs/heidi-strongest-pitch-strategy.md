## The core positioning shift

| Weak (avoid) | Strong (use after traction) |
|---|---|
| "Hire me to build your CPD platform" | "I'm building the category-defining CPD platform. Partner with me or compete against me." |
| Sounds like a job ask | Sounds like a strategic opportunity |
| Small (one course) | Big ($20M+ annual) |
| Easy to say no | Creates urgency + makes them want you |

**The frame**: you're building this either way. Heidi can partner (equity-aligned execution) or you'll scale it independently. Their call.

This frame only works with traction. Without it, "I'm building this either way" sounds like bluffing. With 50+ paying customers and OA endorsement, it's a credible statement.

## The three-act structure

### Act 1 — Hook (cold email to Paul Williamson)

Specific subject: `CPD platform — $6M→$20M revenue opportunity`.

Body opens with:
1. Congratulation on CRO role
2. The platform-economics insight (passive CPD captured in-workflow)
3. The economics ($6M GMV Y1 → $20M Y2)
4. The traction (50 customers, $15k ARR, OA endorsement pending)
5. The competitive angle (Abridge, Nabla, DeepScribe don't have CPD strategy)
6. 20-minute ask + demo URL
7. P.S. with a concrete asset (architecture diagram, integration spec)

### Act 2 — Demo call (show traction + vision)

The deck must answer one question: **How do you make Heidi as un-switchable as an EHR?**

Answer slides:
1. **Problem.** Stickiness — what makes EHR sticky? Locked-in patient records + integrations. Heidi has neither yet.
2. **Answer.** CPD history. Once a clinician has 50+ logged hours via Heidi, they won't switch — losing 3 years of records = liability.
3. **Market.** $480M AU CPD market → $20M Heidi opportunity at 50k user scale.
4. **Moat.** Passive tracking requires in-platform research. Scribe-only competitors can't copy.
5. **Traction.** 50 customers, $15k ARR, OA endorsement pending. Already working.
6. **Economics.** $6M Y1 → $60M Y3 GMV. Heidi 25% commission = $1.5M → $15M ARR.
7. **Enterprise lever.** "Your staff earn CPD by doing their job" closes enterprise sales.
8. **Three options.** Licensing, white-label, employment.

**The key moment in the call**:

> "This is bigger than a course licensing deal. This is a $20M+ annual revenue opportunity that needs an owner. Here are three ways we can structure this..."

### Act 3 — Proposal (three options, bias to employment + equity)

Send `docs/heidi-proposal.md` after the call. The doc lays out:

| Option | Structure | Heidi commitment | Zac upside |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Licensing** | 70/30 rev share, ~$50k Y1 | Lowest | Stays independent, small ceiling |
| **B — White-label** | $150k/yr + per-cert bounty, $150-300k total | Medium | Consulting income, no equity |
| **C — Employment + Equity** | Head of Clinical Education. $140-180k AUD + 0.1-0.3% (4-year vest, 1-year cliff). Own CPD P&L. | Highest | $465k-$1.4M equity at current valuation; $2-6M at realistic exit |

**Your recommendation in the doc**:

> Option C is best because (1) speed — dedicated owner with resources; (2) scale — leverages Heidi's global expansion; (3) moat — 12-18 month lead over competitors; (4) equity — if CPD adds $15M revenue at scale, that's $150M+ in valuation impact.

| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Scarcity** | You're not begging — you're offering first access to an opportunity you're building anyway |
| **Proof** | 50 customers + $15k ARR + OA endorsement = you can execute |

| **Alignment** | Solves Paul's exact CRO problems: ARR growth, retention moat, competitive edge, quick wins |

## Negotiation anchor points

### Equity (most important)

| Stage | Position |
|---|---|
| Initial ask | **0.3%** (4-year vest, 1-year cliff) |
| Likely counter from Heidi | 0.1-0.15% |
| Walk-away floor | **0.1%** |

Math at current valuation (A$700M):
- 0.3% = A$2.1M
- 0.15% = A$1.05M
- 0.1% = A$700k

At realistic exit (A$2-3B, 3-5 years out):
- 0.3% = A$6-9M
- 0.15% = A$3-4.5M
- 0.1% = A$2-3M

### Base salary

| Range | Notes |
|---|---|
| A$140-180k | Market rate for Head of Education at growth-stage SaaS |

Don't negotiate hard on base. Equity is the upside lever.

### Role scope (negotiate firmly)

- **Title:** Head of Clinical Education (not "Director of CPD" — too narrow)
- **Reports to:** Paul Williamson (CRO) or directly to Tom Kelly (CEO) — NOT Dr Condon (means CPD is a revenue function, not a clinical-governance subfunction)
- **P&L ownership:** CPD revenue, costs, margin — measurable line item
- **Team:** 1-2 direct reports by Month 6 (content editor + marketplace ops)
- **Budget:** A$500k Y1 for content + partner acquisition
- **Decision authority:** Course approval, partnership pricing, provider onboarding, CPD policy
- **Equity vest:** 4-year monthly vest, 1-year cliff. Standard.

### The walk-away line

If they offer <0.1% equity:

> "I appreciate the offer, but I can make ~A$150k/year running CEA independently with full ownership of the upside. For me to join Heidi, the equity needs to reflect the value I'm building. At 0.05%, the risk-reward doesn't work. Can we get to 0.1%?"

If they won't move: **walk**. Scale CEA independently. Come back in 12 months with 10× traction and a stronger position.

## What NOT to do

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Ask for VP title immediately | Head of Clinical Education is senior enough; VP signals overreach |
| Demand remote-only if they're hybrid | Lose the role over a benefit, not the equity |
| Push for a large team upfront | Earn budget with results; demanding before delivering is amateur |
| Lead with "I want a job at Heidi" | Lead with "you have a $20M opportunity that needs an owner" |
| Send this pitch without traction | "50 customers, $15k ARR" is the foundation. Without it, the whole frame collapses |
