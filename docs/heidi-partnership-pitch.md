**Author:** Zac Lewis (Concussion Education Australia)

---

## TL;DR

Heidi is the dominant AI scribe in Australia (per public reporting, ~50% of Australian GPs and >2M consultations/week globally) and just closed a US$65M Series B at a ~US$700M valuation. Their published Scribe Usage Policy explicitly offloads jurisdictional, AHPRA, consent and record-keeping compliance onto the practitioner — but Heidi itself provides no CPD-accredited education, no certification, and no published academy. Meanwhile AHPRA's own guidance is that practitioners "must understand any AI tools which they use, to use the tools safely." That is the gap.

CEA has a working, AHPRA-aligned AI-in-clinical-practice course with verifiable certification and a monthly content-refresh pipeline already shipping. The pitch is: Heidi's biggest unaddressed customer-side risk is their users' compliance literacy, and we have the only finished asset in market that closes it.

**Recommended primary framing: White-label CPD ("Heidi Academy, powered by CEA").** Endorse-and-distribute is the soft entry; hire-Zac is the long-tail outcome.

## 1. Heidi's Current Practitioner Education Offerings — What Exists, What Doesn't

| Capability | Exists at Heidi? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Branded learning platform / "Heidi Academy" | **No** | Not present on heidihealth.com; no academy subdomain; no CPD page surfaced in search or site map [1][2] |
| CPD-accredited content (RACGP / Osteo Aus / AHPRA-aligned providers) | **No** | No CPD accreditation referenced anywhere on company or partner pages [1][2] |
| Onboarding training for new users | **Light only** | Product is positioned as "no training required" by competitors and similar tone on Heidi — implicit self-serve onboarding, not formal training [3] |
| Compliance / medicolegal training (AHPRA, APP, TGA-aligned) | **No** | Scribe Usage Policy states practitioner is solely responsible for "licensing, scope-of-practice, informed consent, record-keeping, and patient-confidentiality obligations" — Heidi does not teach them [4] |
| Specialty-specific guidance (GP vs allied health vs mental health) | **No** | Product surfaces support 50+ specialties but no specialty education curriculum is published [1] |
| Certification credentials | **No** | None offered |
| Documented stance on customer compliance responsibility | **Yes — and it pushes all of it to the clinician** | "the final responsibility for all professional and medical decisions related to patient diagnosis, advice, treatment or the quality or accuracy of medical notes rests solely with the medical practitioner" [4] |

**Closest existing educational surface:** Heidi has partnered with GPRA (General Practice Registrars Australia) including "webinars, articles, and workshops" co-marketed through GPRA channels, plus free Heidi Pro for registrars [5]. This is co-marketing, not accredited education. It demonstrates Heidi is willing to wrap education-shaped offerings around member bodies — but no accredited curriculum, no certification.

**Bottom line:** There is no Heidi Academy. There is no CPD. There is a published policy that says compliance is the practitioner's problem. AHPRA says the practitioner must "understand any AI tools which they use" [6]. That is a load-bearing gap.

## 3. Gaps a Partnership Would Fill

**3.1 Heidi's current content emphasis: workflow productivity, not compliance.**
Heidi's homepage and product pages lead with "hours returned to clinicians," "patient visits supported," "ad-free citation-backed evidence" [1]. The compliance posture sits inside legal pages (Safety, Scribe Usage Policy), not in the educational funnel. Safety pages cover *Heidi's* certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA, APP, NHS, GDPR) — but offer almost nothing on what *practitioners* need to know or do [13].

**3.2 Public clinician concerns exist and are documented externally — just not by Heidi.**
AHPRA, Avant, the RACGP-published "Is AI A-OK?" piece (May 2025 AJGP), GPSA's "How to AI Scribes" guide, and the Digital Health Agency's 2025 implementation paper all flag the same things: consent, accuracy verification, record-keeping, medicolegal liability [6][14][15][16]. Heidi has not published a CPD-accredited response to any of it.

**3.3 Competitor education landscape:**
- **Lyrebird Health** markets "no training required" and has a blog-level resource layer; one external article notes CPD automation is "still in development" but not delivered [3][17]. No academy, no certification.
- **Halo Health / Augmedix / Telstra Smart Scribe / Commure** — no Australian CPD-accredited education programs surfaced in search.
- **Net assessment:** No AI scribe vendor in the Australian market currently offers AHPRA-aligned CPD on safe AI use. First mover wins association endorsement and gets cited in every future medicolegal article.

**3.4 The unmet need we'd fill:**
A vendor-branded, CPD-accredited, AHPRA-aligned training and certification track that (a) demonstrably reduces customer compliance risk, (b) gives Heidi an enterprise-procurement and college-endorsement asset, (c) gives individual clinicians a verifiable credential they can show their MDO and practice manager.

## 5. Strategic Options — Three Pitch Framings, Ranked by Likely Close

### Option A — Endorse + Distribute (lowest commitment)
**Likely close: highest. Estimated 6–10 weeks.**

- **What Zac brings:** A finished, AHPRA-aligned course at portal.concussion-education-australia.com/courses/ai-in-clinical-practice with verifiable certificates, 12-month renewal, and a monthly content-refresh pipeline (already operating, evidence in the live admin).
- **What Heidi gets:** A "Recommended Training" link from their docs/onboarding, the ability to point enterprise procurement at an external compliance asset without standing up internal cost, and a public-facing answer to "what about AHPRA?" — without owning the content risk.
- **Deal structure:** Affiliate / co-marketing. Optional discount code for Heidi users. CEA retains content authority, IP, and accreditation. Heidi gets a co-branded landing page.
- **Timeline to close:** First call → MOU in 6–10 weeks. Low legal surface.
- **Key risks:** Easy for Heidi to say "nice, we'll think about it" and stall — no urgency on their side until enterprise procurement pushes back. Mitigation: lead with two specific procurement cases (Monash Health, Beth Israel Lahey) where compliance training will get asked for.

### Option B — White-Label CPD Platform ("Heidi Academy, powered by CEA") (medium commitment)
**Likely close: medium-high. Estimated 4–6 months. Highest strategic upside for both sides.**

- **What Zac brings:** Full content library, certification engine, renewal pipeline, monthly content refresh, accreditation status. Customisation to Heidi's voice, EHR integration patterns, Evidence/Comms product surface, and specialty splits (GP / allied health / mental health / dental / vet).
- **What Heidi gets:** A defensible moat. A "Heidi Academy" page they can ship in Q3 2026. A standing answer to "how do you ensure customers use this safely." An enterprise procurement check-box. A new content marketing surface for top-of-funnel.
- **Deal structure:** Annual license + per-seat or per-cert fee, OR fixed retainer + revenue share on enterprise/multi-seat deals. CEA delivers content, Heidi controls branding and distribution. 18-month minimum term to make content development worthwhile.
- **Timeline to close:** First call → scoped pilot in 3 months → signed in 4–6 months. Likely a 30-day pilot with one specialty (recommend GP given GPRA channel exists [5]).
- **Key risks:** (a) Heidi tries to build in-house once they see the spec — mitigate with exclusivity clause and IP licensing terms; (b) clinical-content sign-off slows things — mitigate by appointing Ben Condon as clinical reviewer with a defined SLA.

### Option C — Hire Zac to Develop the CPD Vertical (highest commitment, biggest upside for Zac)
**Likely close: lowest as a cold pitch. Estimated 6–12 months and usually downstream of Option A or B.**

- **What Zac brings:** Founder-operator track record, CEA as a working proof-of-concept, AHPRA-aligned content authority, clinical credentials (registered osteopath), demonstrated ability to ship.
- **What Heidi gets:** A Director of Practitioner Education who can stand up a CPD vertical as a new revenue line (B2B enterprise training + B2C cert renewals + association co-branding deals) — and who arrives with the asset already built rather than a 12-month build plan.
- **Deal structure:** Director of Practitioner Education / Head of Clinical Education, reporting to Ben Condon. Could be full-time, fractional, or contract-to-hire. CEA continues to operate as a separate brand (or gets acqui-hired into Heidi as the academy unit).
- **Timeline to close:** Almost never closes from a cold pitch. Lands as the third meeting in a sequence that started with Option A or B. Realistically 6–12 months.
- **Key risks:** (a) Heidi doesn't yet think of CPD as a vertical worth owning — mitigate by using Options A/B as the wedge that proves the demand; (b) location/relocation friction (Melbourne HQ); (c) CEA brand entanglement — needs clean separation or acquisition terms.

**Recommended sequencing: pitch Option A and B together in the first call, let Heidi pick. Hold Option C until they've seen the asset deliver.**
