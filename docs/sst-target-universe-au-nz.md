# SST Trainer — AU/NZ Target Universe (researched 2026-07-06)

All clinic names verified on live sources (CCMI locator, ACC provider table,
city searches). Nothing fabricated. Companion to sst-gtm-strategy.md.

## Addressable universe
- **Total: ~500–1,200 clinic locations AU+NZ. High-intent core: ~150–250**
  (concussion-branded + ACC-contracted + vestibular-branded).
- AU concussion-branded clinics: ~40–80 (≈20 CCMI-affiliated + independents).
- AU sports physio actively marketing concussion services: ~300–800 of 9,963
  AU physio businesses (IBISWorld 2026).
- AU vestibular/neuro private physio: ~150–300 practices (~20–40 branded).
- **NZ: 24 private ACC-contracted Concussion Service suppliers** (the premium
  list; Active+ alone runs ~25% of all programmes; 100–200 service sites).
- NZ non-contracted concussion/vestibular physio: ~50–150.

## The three highest-leverage plays
1. **NZ ACC suppliers (24)** — every supplier location MUST staff a
   physiotherapist in the IDT (core-team requirement), and the service spec
   explicitly includes sub-symptom exercise-tolerance work. The physio inside
   each ACC supplier is the buyer; one supplier = many sites. Top: Axis
   Sports Medicine, Active+, TBI Health, Habit Health, ABI Rehabilitation.
2. **AU/NZ multi-site groups** — Lifecare (national chain, runs a CCMI
   service line), Physio Inq (franchise), The Headache Clinic NZ (10 sites),
   Advanced Vestibular Clinics (5 sites + telehealth). One deal, many sites.
3. **CCMI-affiliated clinics: DISQUALIFIED from cold pitch (owner
   2026-07-06)** — platform-locked; the reply is "we use CCMI." Held at
   status=disqualified for a future switch/ecosystem play only. THE ICP IS
   THE TOOL-LESS CLINIC — any clinic managing concussion with verbal
   advice and no supervision tool. CCMI's absence in QLD/SA makes
   Brisbane/Adelaide the densest tool-less ground.

## NZ copy rules (compliance)
- NO Medicare references in NZ-facing copy — ACC framing instead (referral
  via GP/ACC forms, no prior approval on accepted claims, TBI service codes;
  the GP report maps to the ACC written-report obligations).
- NEVER "ACC-endorsed/approved" (ASA Therapeutic & Health Advertising Code —
  implied government endorsement). Safe once true: "used by ACC-contracted
  Concussion Service providers".
- Fair Trading Act: every efficacy claim provable BEFORE publication.
- Device claims in NZ → Medsafe regime (parallel to the TGA caution).
