## Why this framing works

| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| Quotes his own "Care Partner / acts" language | Signals you've actually read what he's said publicly. Differentiates from cold-pitch noise. |
| Leads with **his** opportunity, not your product | Reframes from "buy this" to "you could own this." |
| Free pilot ask | Lowest possible commitment. He doesn't need budget approval, contract, or legal. |
| Specific metric ($1.5-5M ARR) | Anchors the conversation in a number that justifies his time. |
| "Three downstream paths exist but they're downstream" | Removes pressure. Lets him say yes to a pilot without saying yes to anything bigger. |
| Demo URL up front | Lets him verify the build is real before replying. |
