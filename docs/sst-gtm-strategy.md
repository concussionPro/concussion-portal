# SST Trainer — Go-To-Market Strategy (canonical)
**Status: DRAFT — nothing ships until Zac approves this document.** 2026-07-05.

## 1. The one insight everything hangs off
The competitor is not CCMI or Rhea. **The competitor is verbal advice and hope.**
Concussion-literate clinicians already prescribe sub-symptom aerobic exercise —
as a conversation. The patient goes home, and everything until the next review
is recall. Every message meets clinicians inside that existing behaviour and
upgrades it. We never educate them about evidence they already know; we name
the delivery gap they already feel.

## 2. Positioning statement
For clinicians who send concussion patients home with sub-symptom exercise
advice, **SST Trainer is the supervision layer**: it measures each patient's
actual symptom threshold, holds every home session in the prescribed band on
the patient's own watch, and returns the fortnight as decision-ready data —
flares same-day, progression on verified sessions only, clearance on a serial
measured-threshold curve. Unlike age-estimate apps (Rhea, 60% of 220−age) or
in-clinic-only testing (CCMI), supervision continues after the patient leaves.

**Claims discipline (hard rule):** process and visibility claims only.
Recovery-duration outcomes are always attributed to the literature (Leddy
2019; Amsterdam 2023), never to the app.

## 3. Who we pitch (in order)
1. **Course graduates + warm base** — already protocol-trained; the portal
   already teaches the tools as applied science. Founding cohort seed. Zero CAC.
2. **AU concussion/sports physio + osteo clinics** — the 1,137 verified, via
   the existing engine with a dedicated SST sequence (Hub-tier framing).
3. **Baseline-led institutions** — schools/academies get club baseline links
   *through* a local founding clinic (clinic is the hero; institutions create
   the injured-athlete flow back to the clinic).
4. **Worldwide** (post-AU proof): NZ/UK/CA/IE, then US with a claims-language
   pass. CCMI's public clinic map = pre-qualified target list.

- **Offer:** founding partnership — shape it with us, set up now, founding
  terms locked for life. Not a discount; a seat.
- **CTA:** reply "interested". Never links-first, never price in cold email.

## 7. Distribution mechanics
Public app-store listings are shells without a clinic code (activation-key
model; DEMO00 in App Review notes). Nobody finds us via store search — the
clinician handoff (QR / invite email) is the download channel, and stray
store discoveries convert to "ask your clinic", i.e. clinic demand.

## 8. Metrics (doctrine: replies only)
Cold: replies, portal reads (scanner-proof), codes activated.
Activation (the real one): **first patient onboarded per clinic within 14
days** — a clinic with zero patients in 14 days gets the concierge touch.
Never opens/clicks.
