# Module 6 — The AI Practice Hub & Your Certification

You've worked through compliance, tooling, documentation, patient communication, and specialty applications. This final module is about what happens **after** the course — and how the Hub keeps your AI workflow safe, current, and audit-ready, long after you've forgotten the detail of Module 1.

## 1. Welcome to the AI Practice Hub

The AI Practice Hub is the resource library that sits behind your certification. The course teaches you the principles. The Hub is where you go when you're sitting in front of a patient on a Tuesday afternoon and you need a prompt, a template, or a quick check that your favourite tool is still data-resident in Australia.

Inside the Hub you'll find:

- A **prompt library** — copy-paste prompts, categorised by use case and specialty, each with a clinician-review checklist
- A **tool comparison matrix** — refreshed quarterly, showing AU pricing, data residency status, and feature comparison across the AI tools clinicians actually use
- **Document templates** — Word and Google Docs downloads for letters, certificates, education sheets, and consent forms
- A **monthly regulatory update feed** — when AHPRA, OAIC, TGA, or insurer positions shift, you get notified
- A **compliance checklist generator** — AHPRA-aligned audit checklists for your AI workflow

Why it exists: AI tooling moves faster than continuing-education cycles. A static course goes stale in six months. The Hub is the part that doesn't.

**Next step:** Bookmark the Hub URL. You'll come back to it more than the course itself.

## 2. Your Certification

On passing the final quiz, you'll be issued a **Certificate of Completion — AI in Clinical Practice (Australia)**.

A few things to know:

- **Validity: 12 months.** AI regulation in Australia is moving — what is compliant today may be re-scoped by AHPRA or the OAIC inside a year. A short validity window keeps the credential meaningful.
- **Renewal.** Re-sit the short assessment each year to refresh the certificate. (An optional Hub subscription, if you choose to use one, can track your ongoing exposure to the monthly regulatory updates — but it is **not required** to earn, hold, or renew the certificate.)
- **Each certificate carries a unique verifiable URL.** Employers, insurers, and professional indemnity providers can click that URL and confirm the certificate is current — useful when an insurer asks "can you demonstrate AI competency training?"

The certificate is not an AHPRA- or ESSA-issued credential — it is a continuing-education record. Log it that way in your CPD record: AHPRA-registered practitioners record it under their Board's CPD; ESSA-accredited professionals (AEP/AES/ASpS) record it as a CPD activity in their ESSA log.

[KEYPOINT: The certificate is a CPD record, not a regulator credential. Log it under your AHPRA or ESSA CPD as applicable, and check the verifiable URL still resolves before any audit or insurer request.]

**Next step:** Once issued, save the verifiable URL to your CPD folder.

## 3. Using the Prompt Library

The prompt library is organised two ways: **by use case** (documentation, patient communication, referrals, exercise prescription, clinical reasoning, workflow, de-identification) and **by specialty** (physio, osteo, GP, naturopath, mental health, nursing).

Every prompt in the library has:

- A short "when to use" description
- The copy-pasteable prompt text — already containing the standard de-identification preamble
- A list of what PII you must strip before pasting
- A clinician-review checklist for verifying the AI output
- A medicolegal note flagging the specific risk

Use the prompts as a starting point, not a finished product. Every output needs your clinical eye on it before it reaches a patient or a record.

**Next step:** Filter by your specialty, pick three prompts you'd use this week, and trial them against a de-identified scenario.

## 4. The Tool Comparison Matrix

The matrix lists the AI tools we've assessed for Australian clinical use, refreshed each quarter. For each tool you'll see:

- Current AU pricing (per user, per month, including GST where applicable)
- **Data residency status** — where prompts and outputs are stored, and whether the vendor will sign an Australian data-processing agreement
- Feature comparison — scribe quality, template support, integrations with common AU practice management systems
- A tier rating (A / B / C) for clinical safety

Tier A tools are appropriate for clinical content with full de-identification. Tier C tools should not see clinical content at all. The matrix tells you which is which, and updates when vendors change posture.

**Next step:** Confirm your current tool is still rated Tier A before your next session.

## 5. The Monthly Update Feed

When something material changes — an AHPRA position statement, an OAIC guidance note, a TGA decision affecting AI as a medical device, or a major insurer changing what they'll cover — you'll get a short email summary with:

- What changed
- What it means for your practice
- What you need to do (if anything)

No newsletter padding. If nothing material happened that month, you'll get a one-line "no material changes" note.

**Next step:** Whitelist the Hub sender address so updates don't end up in spam.

## 6. Document Templates

The templates folder gives you ready-to-customise Word and Google Docs files for the documents you actually issue:

- Patient education sheets
- Exercise prescriptions
- Return-to-work, return-to-sport, and return-to-school certificates
- Specialist referral letters
- Discharge summaries
- Workers compensation reports
- AI-use patient consent forms
- And more

Every template carries the **compliance attestation footer** — the standard line confirming the document was AI-assisted and clinician-reviewed, with your name, qualification, AHPRA number, and date. Don't strip the footer. It's the line that protects you.

**Next step:** Download two templates relevant to your weekly workflow and customise them with your clinic details.

## 7. The Compliance Checklist Generator

This tool generates an audit-ready checklist for your specific AI workflow. You answer a short set of questions — which tools you use, what data you put in, what consent process you run — and the generator produces a checklist aligned with AHPRA's expectations on documentation, supervision, and patient consent.

Keep the generated checklist in your practice's policies folder. If an insurer, a regulator, or a privacy complainant ever asks "show us your AI governance", that's the document you produce.

**Next step:** Run the generator once now, even if your workflow is informal — you'll surface gaps you didn't know you had.

## 8. The Final Quiz

Ten questions. Multiple choice and short answer. You need 80% to pass.

The questions cover the material from Modules 1–6 — compliance, tooling, documentation, patient communication, specialty workflows, and the Hub itself. You can re-sit immediately if you don't pass first time.

On passing, the certificate is issued automatically and emailed to the address on your account, with the verifiable URL embedded.

**Next step:** When you're ready, hit "Start Quiz". Allow 15 minutes.

## 9. What's Next

You've finished the AI course. A few directions from here:

- **Keep your knowledge current.** AI tooling and regulation shift fast, so whatever you use to stay current — the optional Hub update feed, the regulators' own newsletters (AHPRA/OAIC/TGA), your indemnity carrier's bulletins — put a recurring slot in the calendar to revisit it.
- **Run a 30-day pilot.** Pick one workflow — scribe notes, or patient education, or referrals — and run it with AI assistance for 30 days. Log time saved and any clinical issues that came up. Bring those notes to your next CPD review.
- **Optional tools you may find useful.** Beyond this course, CEA builds practical clinical tools — for example the **Sub-Symptom-Threshold Trainer** (a heart-rate-guided graded-exercise tool for concussion and neuro reconditioning) and the **AI Practice Hub** prompt library and tool-comparison matrix. These are optional resources, available if you want them; they are not required for the course and are not endorsed by AHPRA, ESSA, or any regulator.
- **Consider Concussion Clinical Mastery** — our flagship course (Osteopathy Australia endorsed) on concussion assessment and management: 8 CPD hours online, up to 14 CPD hours when combined with the in-person workshop day. AI workflows accelerate documentation; clinical mastery accelerates outcomes. The two stack. Many clinicians who finish this course go straight into Concussion Clinical Mastery as the next step.

Thanks for working through the course. The work doesn't end at the certificate — it ends every time a patient gets better care because your documentation, communication, and reasoning are sharper. That's the whole point.
