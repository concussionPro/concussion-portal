## What this consent is about

Our clinic uses AI tools to help with parts of your care — most commonly to take notes during your appointment, to help draft letters, and to help prepare patient information sheets. We want you to understand how we use these tools and ask for your consent before we use them in your care.

## What AI tools we use

We currently use: {ai_tools_used}

These tools help with:

- Recording and transcribing what's said in your appointment so we can write clearer notes
- Drafting referral letters, certificates, and patient handouts
- Assisting our clinical reasoning by helping us check our own thinking

## How your information is handled

- **Identifying information** (your name, date of birth, Medicare number, address, phone) is **removed** before any clinical content is processed by these tools, where this is practical.
- **Recordings or notes** from your session are stored in line with our practice privacy policy.
- **Where our AI tools store data:** {data_residency}
- We do not sell your information. We do not use your information to train AI systems.
- You can ask us to delete any AI-generated record at any time.

## Your clinician's responsibility

- Every AI-generated output (note, letter, handout) is **reviewed and signed off by your clinician** before it becomes part of your record or is sent to anyone.
- The AI is an assistant. The clinical decisions, the responsibility, and the duty of care remain with your clinician.

## Your rights

- **You can decline AI use.** This will not affect the care you receive. We will simply take notes the traditional way.
- **You can change your mind at any time.** Just tell us at the start of an appointment.
- **You can ask what was generated.** We will show you any AI-assisted document about you on request.
- **You can request correction or deletion** of any record about you, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
- **You can complain** to our practice, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), or to AHPRA if you have concerns about your care.

## Privacy framework

This consent operates under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Your health information is also protected by state and territory health records legislation where applicable.
