## How a naturopath approaches it

In a naturopathic consultation, your practitioner will consider:

- Your full health picture and any current medications or supplements
- Whether any specific testing might be useful
- Whether dietary change alone may address your situation
- Whether supplementation is appropriate, and at what dose, individualised to you
- How progress will be monitored

Doses, durations, and product choices are individualised — there is no one-size-fits-all approach.

## When to consult your GP

Please consult your GP if you:

- Are taking prescription medications
- Have a chronic health condition
- Are pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive
- Are considering supplementation alongside other treatments
- Notice any new or worsening symptoms
