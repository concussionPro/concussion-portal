## Plan overview

{student_name} is medically advised to follow a graded return-to-school plan in four stages, starting {plan_start_date}. Progression between stages depends on symptom tolerance, not calendar time alone.

---

## Stage 1 — Rest and recovery

**Suggested dates:** {stage_1_dates}

**What the student can do:**
- Light cognitive activity at home (reading, simple tasks for short periods)
- Gentle daytime activity as tolerated

**What to avoid:**
- School attendance
- Screen time beyond brief, low-intensity use
- Physical activity beyond walking
- Driving (if relevant)

**Symptom monitoring:** Parents to log symptoms morning and evening.

**Criteria to progress:** Symptoms stable or improving for 24 hours at rest.

---

## Stage 2 — Gradual return (half-days, modified academic load)

**Suggested dates:** {stage_2_dates}

**What the student can do:**
- Half-days at school (e.g. mornings only)
- Modified academic load — extra time for tasks, fewer concurrent subjects, reduced testing
- Quiet space available for symptom breaks
- Walking and light incidental activity

**What to avoid:**
- Full academic day
- Sport, PE participation
- High-intensity screen work
- Examinations or significant assessments

**Symptom monitoring:** Teaching staff to flag if the student appears symptomatic; parents informed at end of day.

**Criteria to progress:** Tolerates half-days without symptom worsening for 3-5 days.

---

## Stage 3 — Full school days (modified physical activity)

**Suggested dates:** {stage_3_dates}

**What the student can do:**
- Full academic day
- Symptom-tolerated participation in non-contact, non-collision activity
- Catch-up on missed academic content gradually

**What to avoid:**
- Contact sport
- High-impact activity
- Heavy academic catch-up (significant testing) until cleared

**Symptom monitoring:** Daily check-in with parents.

**Criteria to progress:** Full school day tolerated for 5 consecutive days without symptom worsening.

---

## Stage 4 — Full return

**Suggested dates:** {stage_4_dates}

**What the student can do:**
- All normal school activities
- Gradual return to sport per separate return-to-sport plan (if relevant)

**Ongoing monitoring:** If any symptoms recur, step back one stage and contact clinic.
