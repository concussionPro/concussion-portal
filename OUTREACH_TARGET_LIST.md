# Cold Outreach Target List — Concussion Hub Program

Last revised: 2026-06-03 — incorporates regional intelligence about
Optimum Allied Health, BT Physiotherapy, Allsports, SportsCare Canberra,
Peak Physio Newcastle. Replaces prior P1-P6 wave list.

## Positioning rules

- **Metro pitch (Brisbane / Gold Coast / Sydney / Melbourne CBD):**
  bento stats + dashboard screenshot + "private preview portal for
  {clinic} — concussion training scoped to your team." Discipline-aware
  opening line.
- **Regional pitch (Northern Rivers / Coffs / Newcastle / Canberra
  satellites / regional QLD / SA):** "I'll come to you. No travel. Your
  clinic, your cases, your whole team in one day." This is the
  defensible regional positioning no competitor offers.
- **Network pitch (3+ locations):** "One trained clinical model rolled
  out across your network, not a piecemeal CPD spend." Approach the
  national clinical education manager / Director of Allied Health —
  NOT individual branch principals.

## Hard exclusions (do NOT cold-outreach)

- **Allsports Physiotherapy Group** (55 locations, 680 professionals).
  Enterprise sale — direct call to national clinical education manager.
  Cold-sequencing branches will burn the conversation. Path: warm intro
  via APA or SMA conference, not Resend.
- **SportsCare Canberra.** Already a CCMI partner — they have an
  existing concussion infrastructure. Pitching them is noise.
- **Hospital outpatient clinics, sports-medicine specialist groups.**
  Different buying cycle — institutional procurement, not founder-led.
- **Any clinic with <8 clinicians.** Route to public Melbourne workshop
  (or Sydney/Byron demand list). On-site economics don't work below 8.
