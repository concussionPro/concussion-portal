# Cold Outreach Target List — Hub Program

**Scope:** Large physiotherapy practices and exercise rehabilitation groups, 8+ clinicians, multi-disc preferred. **Excludes** hospital networks and sports medicine specialists. Long-term full east coast + Adelaide + regional underserved centers.

**Travel base:** Ballina-Byron Gateway (BNK) + Gold Coast (OOL) airports — cheap east-coast flights make Sydney / Newcastle / Canberra / Melbourne / Adelaide all economically viable for cohort training.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-03

---

## Strategy filters

1. **8+ clinicians total** (physio, EP, osteo, myo/RMT). Under-8 → public workshop redirect per FAQ.
2. **Physio-led OR EP-led OR multi-disc allied health**.
3. **NOT sports medicine specialist clinics** (out of scope — different sales motion).
4. **NOT hospital networks** (out of scope — long procurement).
5. **NOT already concussion-specialised** — if "concussion clinic" / "vestibular rehab" is a primary service on their site, deprioritise.
6. **Approvable contact** — practice principal email/LinkedIn or named senior clinician.

**Regional underserved bonus:** Clinics in regional centers (Tamworth, Wagga, Albury, Bendigo, Ballarat, Townsville, Cairns, Launceston, etc.) where no concussion-trained allied health practice exists within 100km. Higher conversion potential — no competing offer.

## Underserved regional opportunity (P5 + P6 strategic note)

**These markets have no competing offer.** Regional centers like Tamworth, Wagga, Albury, Bendigo, Townsville, Hobart, Launceston have:
- Multi-disc allied health practices with 8-12 clinicians
- Strong contact-sport culture (rugby league, AFL, rugby union)
- No concussion-specialised allied health within 100km
- Long drive to Sydney/Brisbane/Melbourne for any specialist input
- High patient demand (community sport + agricultural injuries + MVAs)

**Pitch hook for regional centers:** *"There's no concussion-trained allied health clinic within 150km of {city}. {clinic_short_name} could be it — not just a service, but the only one in {region}."*

This is a stronger pitch than metro, where any clinic is one of 30 competing. Discipline-aware variant + regional-monopoly framing converts disproportionately well.

**Travel logistics:** Most regional centers reachable via flight + 1-2hr drive. Bundle multiple regional sends per trip — e.g. Tamworth + Armidale + Coffs in one east-coast loop; Bendigo + Ballarat + Geelong in one VIC trip.
