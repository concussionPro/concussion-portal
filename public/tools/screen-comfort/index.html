The science, in full +

Why colour isn’t arbitrary. Photophobia tracks a specific retinal pathway: melanopsin-expressing ipRGCs (peak ≈479 nm) plus cone contributions converge on thalamic trigeminovascular neurons. So a comfort-aware filter has a principled spectral target — not a generic “blue-light block.”

What a webcam can honestly measure. Reflexive squint (palpebral-aperture ratio) and blink are validated, camera-readable correlates of light discomfort. We read them from face landmarks (EAR). They’re comfort proxies, not diagnostics — bidirectional and confounded by load, dryness and fatigue — so fine fixation/saccade metrics below the webcam noise floor are deliberately excluded.

Contrast is the stronger axis. Contrast sensitivity is a validated construct (Pelli-Robson, quick CSF) causally tied to oculomotor performance — higher contrast shortens saccade latency and raises smooth-pursuit gain. The follow-the-light tasks use exactly those effects to probe your response while it tunes contrast and colour.

How it converges. One honest question — comfortable or not? “Uncomfortable” eases the light; “comfortable” edges it crisper. That runs a transformed up-down adaptive staircase (Cornsweet 1962; Levitt 1971) — the family behind Békésy audiometry and quick CSF — anchored by the objective squint/blink signal, which fixes the poor repeatability of subjective-only tint selection. It settles where comfortable and uncomfortable meet: your most comfortable usable setting.

Honest scope. Generic blue-light filters “probably make no difference” (Cochrane 2023); “precision-tint” reading claims are placebo-equivalent. MellowVision personalizes the environment to your stated & observed comfort — it does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. Wellness/accessibility-grade only.

  1. This tab, now — tap Apply to this tab. Your chosen contrast, brightness and tint cast over the whole page immediately, and stay until you reset.
